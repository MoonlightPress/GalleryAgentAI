# Smart Cover Letter — SHIBUYA CAST. Gallery

Tone:
gentle and curious

Recommended Body of Work:
Community / Local Visibility

Core Fit:
SHIBUYA CAST. sits at the intersection of community and design in central Shibuya — a discerning audience open to contemporary accessible art. Urban Tokyo watercolors resonate with the community-oriented programming of this complex.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
