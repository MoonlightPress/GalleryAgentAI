Tone:
restrained and confident

Recommended Body of Work:
Architecture / Memory / Place

Core Fit:
ACC's fellowship explicitly supports Asian artists working across mediums and geographies, making it ideal for GEGYjiji's practice of capturing transient urban moments through watercolor while navigating the dual context of being Chinese-based in Tokyo. The grant's emphasis on cross-cultural artistic exchange directly aligns with her observation-focused daily practice and the themes of memory and disappearing architectural spaces that define her work.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
