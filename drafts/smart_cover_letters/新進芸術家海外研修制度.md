# Smart Cover Letter — 新進芸術家海外研修制度

Tone:
restrained and confident

Recommended Body of Work:
Architecture / Memory / Place

Core Fit:
新進芸術家海外研修制度 is a government grant explicitly for emerging visual artists seeking overseas research funding—no gallery representation required—and your daily watercolor practice and documented focus on urban architecture make you eligible to apply; check the submission deadline and requirements now, then prepare a project proposal framing how overseas research would deepen your work on memory and disappearing moments in built environments.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
