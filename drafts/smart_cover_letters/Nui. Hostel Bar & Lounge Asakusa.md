# Smart Cover Letter — Nui. Hostel Bar & Lounge Asakusa

Tone:
gentle and curious

Recommended Body of Work:
Community / Local Visibility

Core Fit:
Nui. draws Tokyo's creative expat and design community — an international audience who appreciate quiet, considered Japanese urban art. The 'Chinese artist documenting Tokyo' angle is especially resonant here.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
