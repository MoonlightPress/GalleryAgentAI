# Smart Cover Letter — 2026年度 東京芸術文化創造発信助成 カテゴリーII［長期助成］芸術創造活動

Tone:
clear and professional

Recommended Body of Work:
Architecture / Memory / Place

Core Fit:
This is a Tokyo-based long-term grant for visual arts creation—you're already based there, and the deadline and funding structure could anchor a sustained body of work around your daily watercolor practice over 2026. Check the application criteria to confirm they accept individual painters working in watercolor on paper, then gather 8–12 recent urban studies that best represent your current direction before you submit.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
