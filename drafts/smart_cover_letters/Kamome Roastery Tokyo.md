# Smart Cover Letter — Kamome Roastery Tokyo

Tone:
gentle and curious

Recommended Body of Work:
Community / Local Visibility

Core Fit:
Kamome explicitly programs artists with design sensibility. Free exhibition, no upfront cost, 10% sales commission only. Watercolor on rail system fits perfectly. GEGYjiji's 90k Instagram is 150x the required minimum follower count.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
