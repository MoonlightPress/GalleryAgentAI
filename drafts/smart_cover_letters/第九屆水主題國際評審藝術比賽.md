# Smart Cover Letter — 第九屆水主題國際評審藝術比賽

Tone:
restrained and confident

Recommended Body of Work:
General Portfolio

Core Fit:
第九屆水主題國際評審藝術比賽's focus on water as subject matter creates a natural platform for GEGYjiji's architectural watercolors, where water often appears as reflective surface and atmospheric element in her urban Tokyo scenes. The competition's emphasis on the medium itself—watercolor as both technique and conceptual vehicle—directly validates her daily practice of capturing ephemeral light and memory through translucent washes on paper.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
