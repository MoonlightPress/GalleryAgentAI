# Smart Cover Letter — 2026年度 第2期 芸術文化による社会支援助成

Tone:
gentle and curious

Recommended Body of Work:
Architecture / Memory / Place

Core Fit:
This is a Tokyo-based grant for community-engaged visual art—no application fee, and open to un-represented artists—which means your daily watercolor practice and urban focus could be positioned toward a socially engaged project if you have one in mind. Before applying, confirm with the organizers whether painting-based work counts as eligible "community engagement," then sketch out a brief project proposal (even a small series or public sharing initiative around your architectural studies) to include with your submission before the 2026 deadline.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
