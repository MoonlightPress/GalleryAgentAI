restrained and confident

Recommended Body of Work:
General Portfolio

Beijing open residency in Caochangdi — relevant for when GEGYjiji travels to China. Rolling applications, no fixed deadline. Exhibition opportunity included.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
