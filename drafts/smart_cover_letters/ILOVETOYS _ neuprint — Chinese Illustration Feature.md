# Smart Cover Letter — ILOVETOYS / neuprint — Chinese Illustration Feature

Tone:
focused and editorial

Recommended Body of Work:
Artist Book / Printed Matter

Core Fit:
She's Chinese. 'Chinese artist documenting Tokyo through watercolor' is exactly the cross-cultural story these publications want. Near-term opportunity with lower competition than Japanese press.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
