# Smart Cover Letter — 2026年度 第1期 地域芸術文化活動応援助成

Tone:
clear and professional

Recommended Body of Work:
Daily Poetic Photography

Core Fit:
This 2026 regional arts grant explicitly supports watercolor artists doing urban observation work in Tokyo, and has a concrete deadline—worth confirming the submission requirements and whether your daily watercolor practice counts as the core project or supporting material when you review the full guidelines.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
