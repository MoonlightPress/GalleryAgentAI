# Smart Cover Letter — Book and Sons

Tone:
gentle and curious

Recommended Body of Work:
Artist Book / Printed Matter

Core Fit:
A design-led bookstore with exhibition space and rolling consignment, reachable directly via Instagram — a Tier-1 way to put printed work in front of a book-oriented Tokyo audience. The listing is thin on terms, so the first move is a direct inquiry, not preparation.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
