# Smart Cover Letter — 新進芸術家海外研修制度（特別）

Tone:
clear and professional

Recommended Body of Work:
Architecture / Memory / Place

Core Fit:
This government fellowship supports emerging visual artists conducting overseas research—watercolor illustration qualifies—and offers up to ¥1.5M, making it substantial enough to fund an extended study residency abroad if your daily practice has developed a specific research direction you want to deepen outside Japan. Check the submission deadline and requirements on their official page, then map out whether a focused overseas research proposal (tied to your urban/architectural work) would strengthen your application before you submit.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
