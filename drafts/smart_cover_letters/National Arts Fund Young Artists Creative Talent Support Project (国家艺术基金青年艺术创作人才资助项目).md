# Smart Cover Letter — National Arts Fund Young Artists Creative Talent Support Project (国家艺术基金青年艺术创作人才资助项目)

Tone:
clear and professional

Recommended Body of Work:
General Portfolio

Core Fit:
Supports young Chinese artists across multiple disciplines including painting; applicant is a Chinese national age 26 working in watercolor and illustration.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
