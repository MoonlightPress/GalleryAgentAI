# Smart Cover Letter — 新進芸術家海外研修制度（Emerging Artists Overseas Training Program）

Tone:
restrained and confident

Recommended Body of Work:
Architecture / Memory / Place

Core Fit:
The program's focus on international research and professional development abroad directly supports your practice of documenting ephemeral urban moments and architectural details—allowing you to develop new watercolor methodologies while observing how memory and place manifest in different cities beyond Tokyo. As an early-stage artist with a daily practice rooted in specific geographies, this funding structure enables you to deepen your conceptual investigation of disappearing environments on an international scale.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
