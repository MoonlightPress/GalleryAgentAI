# Smart Cover Letter — 芸術家海外研修制度（Artists' Overseas Training Program）

Tone:
clear and professional

Recommended Body of Work:
General Portfolio

Core Fit:
Government arts training grant for artists and researchers; supports overseas professional development for visual artists including painters and illustrators.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
