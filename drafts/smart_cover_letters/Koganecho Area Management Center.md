# Smart Cover Letter — Koganecho Area Management Center

Tone:
gentle and curious

Recommended Body of Work:
Community / Local Visibility

Core Fit:
Your architectural documentation practice and interest in vernacular Asian spaces aligns with their urban revitalization mission, though the community-engagement emphasis may push you outside pure observation mode.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
