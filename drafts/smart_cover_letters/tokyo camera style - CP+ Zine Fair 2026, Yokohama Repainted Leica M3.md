Tone:
focused and editorial

Artist Book / Printed Matter

Core Fit:
tokyo camera style - CP+ Zine Fair 2026, Yokohama Repainted Leica M3... fits because zines and art-book contexts suit quiet works-on-paper, small editions, atmosphere, and visual sequencing.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
