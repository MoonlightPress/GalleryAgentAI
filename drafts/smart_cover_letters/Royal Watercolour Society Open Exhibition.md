# Smart Cover Letter — Royal Watercolour Society Open Exhibition

Tone:
restrained and confident

Recommended Body of Work:
Artist Book / Printed Matter

Core Fit:
Direct medium match — the RWS open exhibition is the most prestigious international platform for watercolor watercolor / works on paper, and acceptance is a major CV milestone.

Draft Structure:

1. Short greeting
2. Brief introduction
3. Mention why the venue/publication specifically fits the work
4. Mention the body of work
5. Offer portfolio/PDF/Instagram
6. Thank them briefly

Important:
- Do not make the message too long.
- Avoid sounding desperate.
- Avoid generic praise.
- Match the emotional tone of the institution.
