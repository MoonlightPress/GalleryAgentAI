**Consequence**: Excluded from fine art galleries, underpriced in illustration market

**Mitigation**:
- Clarify medium (original painting = fine art pricing; digital = illustration pricing)
- Choose representation carefully (avoid pure illustration galleries AND overly commercial art poster galleries)
- Maintain separate portfolio for commissioned illustration vs. gallery fine art
- Price originals at fine art rates ($3K+ for mid-size works), prints at accessible rates
