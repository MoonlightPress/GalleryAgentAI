THE LIVING INTERFACE AND SAFFRON'S THREE INTELLIGENCE LAYERS
Bible Entry 11 — Written 2026-06-02

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT COVERS

Two things, which connect.

The first is the hero image system — how each companion's page greets the artist
when she arrives, and why that greeting should change. The second is the full
specification of Saffron's page: not just what she shows, but how her three
intelligence layers work and how they depend on each other.

These are not unrelated. The hero rotation system is about what the interface
feels like to arrive at. Saffron's layers are about what she has gathered while
the artist was away. Both are expressions of the same conviction: the system
should be alive. It should feel like visiting somewhere, not opening a file.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PART ONE: THE LIVING HERO SYSTEM

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE PROBLEM WITH A STATIC BANNER

A hero image that never changes is wallpaper. The artist sees it the first time
and it registers. She sees it the second time and it is familiar. She sees it
the tenth time and she does not see it anymore.

This matters more than it sounds. The companions are not features. They are
characters in a relationship. Characters in a relationship change. They have
moods. They look different in morning light than in evening light. The atelier
in January is not the atelier in September. A static banner makes the companions
feel like product mascots instead of presences.

The solution is not cosmetic variety for its own sake. The images should respond
to context: time of day, season, weather. An artist opening the page at six in
the morning should feel something different than opening it at eleven at night.
The system should seem to know what time it is.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE FOLDER STRUCTURE

Each companion has its own image pool. Images live at:

  frontend/src/assets/heroes/mochi/
  frontend/src/assets/heroes/peppercorn/
  frontend/src/assets/heroes/saffron/

Adding an image to a folder immediately includes it in rotation. No registration,
no configuration, no code change. The system discovers images from the folder
contents at build time using import.meta.glob.

Current state: one image per companion. This is the starting condition. It is
enough to establish the system. The value grows as the pool grows.

Target: 3-5 images per companion within the first months of use. 100 per
companion eventually. At 100, the rotation is rich enough that the artist rarely
sees the same image twice in a week. The early images establish what good looks
like. Each addition should meet the same standard.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE SELECTION ALGORITHM

Selection is not random. Random is artless. Selection is weighted by context.

The algorithm works in four layers, applied in order:

  1. ROTATION COMPLETENESS
     No image repeats until the full pool has been shown once. This is the base
     constraint. Even with weighting, the rotation completes a full cycle before
     any image appears twice. The artist will eventually see everything.

  2. TIME-OF-DAY BIAS
     Images are tagged by filename as morning, midday, afternoon, evening, or
     night. The algorithm weights toward images matching the current time bracket.
     A dawn image surfaces more often before nine in the morning. An evening image
     surfaces more often after seven at night. Images without time tags are eligible
     at all hours with neutral weight.

  3. SEASONAL WEIGHT
     Images tagged by season — spring, summer, autumn, winter — are weighted up
     when the current calendar month matches. A snowbound interior appears more
     often in December and January. A warm afternoon light image appears more
     often in July and August. Untagged images remain eligible throughout the year.

  4. WEATHER AWARENESS (CONDITIONAL)
     If a weather API is available, images tagged to weather conditions — clear,
     overcast, rain, snow — receive a weight multiplier when the local forecast
     matches. On a grey overcast morning, overcast-tagged images are favored over
     bright summer images. This layer is optional. The system degrades gracefully
     when no weather data is present. Weather tagging is low priority until the
     pool is large enough to make it meaningful; with five images, it is noise.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FILE NAMING CONVENTION

Tags are embedded in the filename, separated by underscores:

  mochi_morning_spring_01.png
  mochi_evening_02.png
  mochi_winter_overcast_01.png
  mochi_03.png                    ← no tags, eligible always

The selection system reads the filename, extracts recognized tags from a fixed
vocabulary, and applies weights accordingly. Unknown tokens are ignored. The
convention is additive — more specific images get narrower but more contextually
resonant placement.

Recognized time-of-day tags: morning, midday, afternoon, evening, night
Recognized season tags:       spring, summer, autumn, winter
Recognized weather tags:      clear, overcast, rain, snow

Tag order within the filename does not matter.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NO TEXT ON THE HERO IMAGE. EVER.

This is an absolute rule, not a preference.

The hero image belongs to the companion. It is the companion's face, their mood,
their light. No text overlay, no caption, no UI element sits on top of it. No
greeting text. No status message. No card. Nothing.

Text overlays on hero images are a compromise. They emerge from the assumption
that the banner is wasted space unless it carries information. This assumption
is wrong. The image carries information — it carries the feeling of arriving
somewhere. Text placed on top of it fights the image for attention and always
degrades the image more than the text is worth.

Mochi's current page places a focus card overlaid on the hero. That focus card
should migrate below the banner, not sit on top of it. The card's content is
important and correct. Its placement on the image is not. When the Three
Companions rebuild happens, the hero is clean on all three pages.

Saffron's page is already correct on this point. Peppercorn's page is not.
This rule holds even when the pool is small. Even with one image, the image
is clean. Clean is the standard from the first image to the hundredth.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PART TWO: SAFFRON'S THREE INTELLIGENCE LAYERS

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT SAFFRON IS NOT

Before describing what Saffron does, it is useful to describe what she is not.

Saffron is not a dashboard. She is not a row of metric cards — "163 opportunities
found, 4 actionable, 12 applications submitted." She is not a comparison engine
that measures the artist against benchmarks and implies she is behind. She is not
a content aggregator that surfaces art world news for its own sake.

These are things an analytics tab might do. Saffron is not an analytics tab.

Saffron sees patterns from altitude. A statistic at ground level tells you how
many. A pattern from altitude tells you what it means. Saffron's value is in
interpretation — not false interpretation, not invented significance, but the
kind of context that only becomes visible when you step back far enough.

She does not advise. She describes. The artist looks at what Saffron describes
and draws her own conclusions. Saffron sees the patterns from above so the
artist does not have to.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE THREE LAYERS

Saffron's page is organized into three intelligence layers. They are not tabs.
They are successive depths of context — each one deeper than the last, each one
building on the previous. They can be read in sequence or navigated by depth,
but they are not independent modules. They tell one story in three chapters.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LAYER ONE: MARKET INTELLIGENCE

What it answers: "What kind of world is this artist working in?"

Market Intelligence describes the opportunity landscape as a shape, not a list.
The content:

  CATEGORY BREAKDOWN
  What kinds of opportunities dominate the current landscape? Open calls versus
  residencies versus consignment venues versus fairs versus prizes. How has the
  mix shifted over the last six months? Is there a category that has grown
  recently or gone quiet?

  TOKYO VS. INTERNATIONAL SPLIT
  How many actionable opportunities exist in Tokyo, in Japan outside Tokyo, and
  internationally? For this artist at this career stage, Tokyo is primary. The
  international picture matters for context and for stretch targets — Cité
  Internationale des Arts, the RWS open exhibition, Printed Matter — which
  are tracked here even when they are not yet actionable.

  SEASONAL PATTERNS IN OPEN CALLS
  Which institutions announce their calls in March? Which have a reliable autumn
  window? Saffron builds this picture over time. After two years of pipeline data
  she can say with genuine confidence: expect three new watercolor open calls to
  open in September — they arrive every year. The seasonality becomes a planning
  tool: the artist can prepare in August for what arrives in September.

  TREND DETECTION
  Are more or fewer residencies becoming available in Japan? Is the Tokyo
  watercolor community growing its exhibition presence? Is interest in quiet
  observational work rising or receding in the institutions she targets? These
  are slow-moving patterns that only become visible over months. Saffron
  watches for them without being asked.

What this layer does for the artist: she arrives knowing the shape of the
landscape. She does not need to discover that October has more open calls than
July — Saffron has already watched for that. The artist spends her attention on
decisions, not on orientation.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LAYER TWO: ARTIST INTELLIGENCE

What it answers: "How does my work and career fit into the larger picture?"

Artist Intelligence describes the artist in context, not in isolation. This is
the most sensitive layer. It requires Saffron to draw comparisons — and
comparisons can feel like judgments. The tone must hold the distinction
consistently: comparisons are for orientation, not evaluation. Saffron describes.
She does not rank. She does not say "you are behind." She says "here are artists
doing adjacent work in similar circumstances."

The content:

  COMPARABLE ARTISTS
  Three to five artists doing similar work in similar career circumstances.
  Not necessarily the same medium or geography, but the same kind of career:
  emerging, building exhibition history, working in a quiet and observational
  mode, engaged with urban environments or interiority. Not superstars. Peers.

  Saffron describes what these artists are doing. Where they are showing. What
  their recent trajectory looks like. Not to induce comparison anxiety — to
  answer the question the artist rarely gets to ask: am I unusual, or is what I
  am doing a recognizable path?

  CAREER TRAJECTORY ANALYSIS
  At this career stage, with this exhibition history, with this kind of work —
  what do the next two years typically look like for an artist in this position?
  Not a prescription. A description of what has happened for others who started
  where she is starting. The trajectory is drawn from real careers Saffron has
  observed, not from a generalized model of artist career development.

  HER WORK IN CONTEXT
  What is the critical and curatorial conversation around work like hers? Urban
  environments, architecture, memory, absence, atmosphere, quiet observation —
  is there a movement, an emerging curatorial interest, a set of peer institutions
  that have been programming this kind of work? Saffron does not evaluate the
  work. She reports on the context the work exists in. This is the intelligence
  a gallerist friend would give the artist over coffee.

  SCORE TRENDS
  Is the pipeline's understanding of her work improving? Are the opportunities
  surfaced this quarter more relevant than last quarter? Are the fit scores
  drifting up or down? This is diagnostic more than intelligence — but Saffron
  is the right place for it. She is the one with the long view.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LAYER THREE: STRATEGIC PATHWAYS

What it answers: "If I want to get somewhere specific, what does that path look like?"

This is the deepest and most ambitious layer. It is also the most dependent on
Peppercorn — without the artist's stated goals and preferences, the pathways
cannot be personalized. Generic pathways are listicles. Personalized pathways
are maps.

THE STRUCTURE OF A PATHWAY

A pathway is not a to-do list. A to-do list says: do this, then this, then this.
It implies the path is known and the only question is execution. Pathways
acknowledge that the path is not fixed — it has several routes, several timescales,
several dependencies that are partly under the artist's control and partly not.

A pathway surfaces:

  - The current position: what the artist has and has not yet done that is
    relevant to this goal
  - The milestones: intermediate markers that have historically preceded this
    kind of outcome for artists in similar positions
  - The dependencies: what needs to be true for the next milestone to be
    reachable — which may include things the system cannot control
  - The realistic timeframe: not a deadline, but a range derived from comparable
    careers, with the factors that affect where in the range she lands
  - What would accelerate it: specific actions with specific leverage on the
    timeline, ranked by impact

THE THREE SPECIFIED PATHWAYS

Three pathways are specified by the artist:

  1. PATH TO 1M INSTAGRAM FOLLOWERS
     This is a distribution and visibility pathway, not a gallery pathway. It
     requires different activities: consistent posting rhythm, community
     engagement, an identifiable visual voice across bodies of work, meaningful
     engagement with adjacent accounts. The pipeline is not optimized for this —
     Instagram growth is not an opportunity in the traditional sense. But Saffron
     can model it: what artists with similar work have done to build large
     followings, what the realistic timeframes look like at different levels of
     posting consistency, and how Instagram visibility intersects with exhibition
     opportunity. The path to 1M is not separate from the path to a major gallery —
     they share the visibility layer.

  2. PATH TO MAJOR GALLERY BY 30
     This is a long-horizon institutional pathway. Major gallery means here a
     well-regarded commercial gallery with an international program and artist
     roster — not just a respected local venue. The path runs through: group
     exhibition history at institutional spaces, residency or fellowship credits,
     publication presence, relationship building with curators and collectors over
     several years. Saffron models this as a four-to-six year arc from the current
     position. She is not describing a sequence of deterministic steps. She is
     describing the territory between here and there, and marking which sections
     require relationships that cannot be rushed.

  3. PATH TO FIRST PUBLICATION
     The most near-term of the three. A zine or artist book — not a major
     monograph, but a real publication that enters circulation and lives in shops
     and collections. The milestones include identifying distribution venues for
     consignment (Daikanyama T-Site, POST, Village Vanguard, international
     distros like Printed Matter and Idea Books), selecting or developing a body
     of work suitable for print format, navigating short-run printing logistics,
     and submitting to an artist book fair such as Offprint Tokyo. This is
     achievable within the current tier framework without waiting for
     institutional credentialing. It is the right first publication move.

These three are not the only pathways Saffron can hold. They are the first three —
the ones the artist has named. New pathways can be defined through Peppercorn, or
suggested by Saffron when she observes that the artist's recent activity implies
a direction she has not yet named.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

HOW PEPPERCORN FEEDS SAFFRON

Strategic Pathways are Saffron's most personalized content. They cannot be
generic. A generic pathway to a major gallery by 30 is a listicle. A personalized
pathway is built from this body of work, this exhibition history, these
relationship targets already tracked in the system, this specific city and
community, these specific venues the artist has already followed or dismissed.

The personalization comes from Peppercorn.

Every time the artist answers a question on Peppercorn's page — clarifies a goal,
confirms a preference, records a milestone — Peppercorn writes to shared state.
Saffron reads that state when building pathways. The cycle:

  1. The artist tells Peppercorn her goal: "I want to show at a major gallery
     by 30."
  2. Peppercorn records the goal with target date and current career state.
  3. Saffron reads the goal, the current exhibition history, the submission
     record, the followed venues, and the not-for-me dismissals.
  4. Saffron builds a pathway: here is the gap between where you are and where
     you want to be, here are the intermediate milestones that have preceded this
     kind of outcome, here is what the timeline looks like given your current
     position.
  5. Mochi surfaces the next actionable step from the pathway in Today's Focus.

The loop is: Peppercorn collects the artist's voice. Saffron interprets it from
altitude. Mochi acts on it at ground level. The three companions are not
independent pages. They are a single system with three entry points.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT SAFFRON NEVER DOES IN PATHWAYS

Saffron does not tell the artist whether her goals are realistic. She describes
what the path looks like. The artist decides whether to walk it.

Saffron does not compare the artist unfavorably to others on the same path.
"Artists who reached a major gallery by 30 typically had X" is information.
"You don't have X" is a judgment. Saffron provides the former only.

Saffron does not generate urgency. No countdowns, no "you have 847 days until
your 30th birthday." Timescales exist as context, not pressure.

Saffron does not revise the artist's goals without being asked. If the artist
said she wants a major gallery by 30, that is what Saffron models. She might
note when a pathway requires acceleration to remain realistic — but she does
not quietly update the goal to something more achievable and model that instead.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE THREE LAYERS

The layers compound.

Market Intelligence describes the landscape. Artist Intelligence describes where
the artist stands in it. Strategic Pathways describe how to move through it
toward a named destination.

An artist looking at a pathway without market context does not know if she is
looking at a realistic path or an idealized one. An artist looking at comparable
careers without her own position in view cannot tell whether the comparison is
flattering or concerning or simply informative. The layers are designed to be
read in sequence, or navigated by depth — but reading only one layer without
the others is reading a chapter without the book.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR CODE

HERO IMAGE SYSTEM

The hero rotation utility exists at:

  frontend/src/utils/heroImages.js

Currently it uses import.meta.glob with eager loading and a single random pick.
The next iteration of this utility adds:

  - Filename tag parsing: extract recognized tags from the filename string
  - Time-of-day bracket detection: client-side, using Date.getHours()
  - Weighted selection: replace pure random with a weighted pick where matching
    tags multiply the probability of selection
  - Rotation completeness tracking: use sessionStorage to record which images
    have been shown; reset on true page load, not navigation
  - Weather weight multiplier: deferred until the pool is large enough to
    benefit; irrelevant with three images per folder

The function signature stays the same from the consumer's perspective. The
named exports — mochiHero, peppercornHero, saffronHero — remain. Weighting is
internal. Components do not need to change when the algorithm improves.

SAFFRON'S THREE LAYERS

None of the three layers exist in code yet. Implementation follows the data
dependency chain:

  Market Intelligence is the most pipeline-adjacent. Much of the raw data
  already exists in memory/opportunities.json and the pipeline output files.
  A new engine reads those files and produces a structured market summary.
  This is buildable without new data sources. It is the right first Saffron step.

  Artist Intelligence requires the comparable artists system and the submission
  history (which depends on the Applied card action specified in Bible Entry 10).
  It cannot be fully built until Applied has accumulated data. Stub the UI now;
  populate it later.

  Strategic Pathways require Peppercorn's goal intake to exist. They cannot be
  personalized without the artist's stated goals. Build the goal intake in
  Peppercorn first. Build Pathway generation in Saffron second. Build the
  Mochi surfacing of pathway actions third. The three steps are one feature
  split across three pages.

The Saffron page currently shows a clean hero banner with no content below it.
That is correct. Do not add placeholder content for the three layers until the
data layer is ready. A placeholder with no real data is worse than a beautiful
empty page. Saffron has nothing to report until she has something to report.
The empty page is honest. Fake data is not.

A NOTE ON SEQUENCE

Bible Entry 08 described the Three Companions architecture and established the
companion characters. Bible Entry 10 described the four card feedback actions
and how they route to each companion. This entry describes the living hero system
and the full specification of what Saffron sees.

Together they define what the system becomes.

The right build order, given all three entries:

  1. Applied (Bible 10) first — submission history begins to accumulate
  2. Market Intelligence (this entry) second — pipeline data is already present
  3. Peppercorn goal intake — artist's voice enters the system
  4. Artist Intelligence — comparable artists, trajectory analysis
  5. Strategic Pathways — the three layers are now connected
  6. Hero pool growth to 3-5 per companion — the living interface begins
  7. Weighted selection with time-of-day and seasonal bias
  8. Hero pool growth to 100 per companion — the rotation is fully alive

Nothing in this entry is implemented. This is the specification. When the time
comes to build, start here.

ADDENDUM: PEPPERCORN ALTERNATIVE CONCEPT — ANIMATED CARTOON

There is a second visual direction for Peppercorn that is worth preserving even
though it is not the current plan.

The alternative concept: Peppercorn as a fully illustrated cartoon character,
animated, shown doing different activities based on time of day. He is cooking
in the morning. He is playing guitar in the afternoon. He is reading in the
evening. He is sleeping at night. The activities change on their own. The page
feels inhabited — you arrive and find him mid-something, rather than posed.

This is a meaningful departure from the watercolor atelier aesthetic that governs
the current system. The watercolor approach treats all three companions as part
of the same visual world — warm, analogue, painterly. Animated cartoon Peppercorn
would have his own register entirely: more playful, more expressive, potentially
more endearing to a specific kind of user who responds to character animation
over illustration.

The two approaches are not compatible within the same interface. Choosing the
cartoon direction for Peppercorn would mean either extending that register to
Mochi and Saffron (a larger commitment) or accepting a visual discontinuity
between the three pages (probably wrong).

The current plan is the watercolor version. Build that first. The watercolor
approach is coherent with the existing Mochi illustration, with the established
CSS palette, and with the quiet observational tone the system has throughout.
It is the lower-risk path and the more consistent one.

The cartoon concept is documented here so it is not lost. If the watercolor
Peppercorn page is built and it feels too static — if the artist opens it and
does not feel that Peppercorn is present, only depicted — the animated direction
is the right thing to revisit. The test is whether the page feels inhabited.
The watercolor version should clear that bar. If it does not, come back to this.

ADDENDUM: SAFFRON INTENDED FINAL VISUAL STYLE — CLEAN VECTOR

Unlike the Peppercorn cartoon concept, which is an alternative to consider later,
the vector direction for Saffron is the intended final style. This is not a
"maybe." It is a "not yet."

The concept: clean vector illustration, editorial in feel, flat warm colors built
from geometric forms. Sophisticated and somewhat abstract — closer to a well-art-
directed magazine spread than to the warm painterly atelier of Mochi's page.
Saffron herself as an elegant vector silhouette: precise, composed, high above
everything, looking out. The page feels like a well-designed annual report made
by someone with genuine taste — structured, clear, unhurried.

Data visualizations on this page are native to the aesthetic, not clinical
intrusions. A category breakdown does not look like a default Chart.js bar chart
dropped into a warm interface and hoping nobody notices. It looks like it was
drawn by the same hand that drew Saffron. Flat geometric forms, warm palette,
information that reads as designed rather than generated. The difference matters
because Saffron's page is the most data-heavy of the three, and data that looks
out of place makes the whole page feel unstable.

WHY THIS DIVERGES FROM MOCHI

Mochi's page is an atelier. It is warm, painterly, domestic — the artist arrives
and things are prepared. That aesthetic is correct for a page whose emotional
register is safety and readiness. It would be wrong for Saffron.

Saffron is the observatory. She perches above the landscape. Her page should feel
like altitude — clear air, wide view, clean geometry. The warmth is still present
in the palette (this is not a cold minimal tech aesthetic), but the visual
language shifts from brushstroke to vector, from domestic to elevated. The shift
is intentional. Each companion page should feel distinct, not like three tabs of
the same interface.

The watercolor placeholder currently on Saffron's page is correct for now. It
establishes the space. It is not the final answer.

WHAT SAFFRON THE SILHOUETTE LOOKS LIKE

A vector bird silhouette, not a detailed illustration. The silhouette is
immediately recognizable as a bird — the posture of a perched raptor or large
songbird, head turned slightly, alert — but rendered in clean flat geometry
rather than illustrated feathers. Warm red or amber, the colors established in
Bible Entry 08. The silhouette is large enough to be a presence on the page, not
a logo tucked into a corner.

The data visualizations, the three intelligence layers, and the silhouette share
the same visual vocabulary. Geometric, flat, warm, editorial. Nothing on this
page should look like it came from a charting library or a UI component kit
without modification.

PRIORITY

Current priority is content, not styling. Saffron's three intelligence layers —
Market Intelligence, Artist Intelligence, Strategic Pathways — do not exist in
code yet. Building them is the work. The vector visual treatment is the final
layer, applied after the content is real and the page is alive with actual data.

The sequence: build the content first, style it last. Do not let the styling
ambition block the content work, and do not let the placeholder styling become
permanent through inertia.
