## What It Is

torch press is an independent book label based in Tokyo (Yoyogi, Shibuya-ku), founded in December 2013 by editor Nao Amino. It publishes art books and photobooks — primarily photography-focused but with a significant strand of contemporary painting, performance documentation, and conceptual art. Positioned clearly in the international fine art publishing tier: their books are reviewed by Collector Daily, stocked by Dashwood Books (NYC), distributed in Europe through Idea Books, and designed by figures like Hans Gremmen. This is not a zine press or self-publishing service — it is an editorially curated label producing high-production-value limited-run monographs and exhibition catalogs.

## Who Runs It

**Nao Amino** — founder and sole editor. He works "centered on editing, around art and photography." He is the singular decision-maker on which artists and projects torch press develops. There is no editorial board, no submission committee. All projects are relationships.

## Aesthetic Pattern

The through-line across the catalog is not medium but **quality of attention**. The unifying aesthetic is:

- **Quiet observation** — work that asks the viewer to slow down and look closely
- **Light as subject** — from Kawauchi's luminous grain to Sambe's still-life transparencies to Nakajima's light-focused painting
- **Intimate scale** — domestic spaces, everyday objects, the near-at-hand
- **Presence and absence** — what remains, what has passed, what is not quite visible
- **Materiality** — how things are made, how surfaces hold or reflect light
- **High production values** — the book itself as an aesthetic object

The Rinko Kawauchi dimension is significant: she is the most prominent artist in the torch press catalog, and her visual world (soft grain, luminous everyday subjects, domestic and natural, quiet emotion) is the closest photographic equivalent to thoughtful atmospheric watercolor. The overlap is aesthetic philosophy, not medium.

**GEGYjiji fit:** Urban environments, architecture, memory, absence, atmosphere, quiet observation — this maps closely onto torch press's catalog sensibility. The visual fit score is accurate. The aesthetic is genuinely aligned.

## Submissions — Critical Finding

**There is no submissions page. There is no open call. torch press does not accept unsolicited work.**

This is a curated label where the editor (Nao Amino) initiates all relationships. The only public-facing email (`order@torchpress.net`) is a sales/order address, not an editorial contact. The pattern across the catalog — Kawauchi, Morimura, Twombly — indicates projects are developed through existing relationships in the Tokyo and international art world.

**Cold email is not the access model here.** Sending work to order@torchpress.net would be the equivalent of sending a portfolio to a gallery's sales desk.

## Realistic Pathway (Long-Term)

The realistic route to torch press is not a cold submission — it is becoming visible in the ecosystem where Nao Amino already operates:

1. **TABF presence** — torch press participates in Tokyo Art Book Fair (MOT). Being an exhibitor, or having work stocked by a distributor who tables there, creates adjacency.
2. **Published elsewhere first** — torch press artists typically have exhibition histories with significant institutions. A record of publications with smaller respected presses makes a future conversation possible.
3. **Art book ecosystem connections** — On Reading (Nagoya, where their 10th anniversary was held), shashasha, Dashwood — these are nodes in the same network.
4. **Warm introduction** — The likeliest access point is someone who already knows Nao Amino making an introduction. This is a single editor who personally selects every project; relationships matter more than portfolios here.

**Realistic timing:** Post Tier 3 (small institutional shows, juried recognitions, a couple of publications with smaller presses). The deep-work year at 30+ that the tier framework envisions is when this becomes a realistic conversation.
