## Japan/Asia-Specific: A Honest Assessment

Dedicated open anthology calls specifically targeting Japan or Asia-Pacific artists in the visual art/watercolor space are rare in the public call ecosystem. The Japan small press world operates differently:

- **合同誌 (gōdōshi) / anthology zine culture** exists primarily within artist communities on pixiv and Twitter — it's by mutual invitation, not public open call. A painter with 90k Twitter followers is in a position to be invited into these circles, but the entry point is relationship-building, not application.

- **The right Japan-specific strategy is not open calls but presence**: exhibiting at ZINEフェス東京, getting work stocked at MOUNT ZINE, appearing at TABF — these create the connections that lead to anthology invitations.

- **Asia-Pacific art publications** (ArtAsiaPacific, etc.) are journalism and criticism, not artist anthology calls.

- **Broken Pencil** (Canadian zine magazine and resource hub — brokenpencil.com) maintains a running list of international zine anthology calls and is worth monitoring. They have no Japan focus but regularly list calls accessible to international artists.
