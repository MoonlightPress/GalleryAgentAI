## Eligibility & Rules

- Maximum **2 people per booth**
- **Creators must sell directly** — no consignment sales permitted
- Items must be **hand-carried via public transit** — no postal/delivery submissions
- Prohibited: explicit (エログロ), specific political or religious content
- Works must be original and created by the exhibitor

## Fit Assessment (GEGYjiji)

**Tier 1 — strong fit.**

- Low barrier, no jury: pay fee, show up, sell
- Recurring event (BOOK CULTURE CLUB runs this monthly/bimonthly) — low-risk way to establish presence
- Asakusa venue is established on the Tokyo zine circuit
- Aligns with zine/small press work in the current Tier 1 strategy
- ¥5,200 total cost for solo with promotion is accessible

**One concern:** The "hand-carry only" rule means no advance shipping. Artist must transport work herself on the day.
