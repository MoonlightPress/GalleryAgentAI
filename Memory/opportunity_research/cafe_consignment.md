# Tokyo Café Consignment & Wall Display — Research
## Shimokitazawa, Nakameguro, Koenji

**Research date:** 2026-06-01  
**Focus:** Cafés and café-gallery hybrids that actively display artist work, accept consignment, or host rotating wall exhibitions. Direct contact info where found.

---

## How to Read This

"Consignment" in the Tokyo café context means one of three things:
1. **Permanent wall display with sale** — work hangs on the walls, café takes a commission when pieces sell
2. **Rotating exhibitions** — artist takes a dedicated period (1–4 weeks) of wall space; café may take a small commission or flat rental fee
3. **Zine/print consignment** — small printed works or zines left for sale at the counter

All three are relevant for GEGYjiji's Tier 1 strategy. The distinction matters for outreach: for type 1-2, propose an exhibition period; for type 3, bring work and ask about stocking terms.

Even if they don't formally accept consignment, the relationship is worth building — this is the kind of venue where work would look genuinely at home.
