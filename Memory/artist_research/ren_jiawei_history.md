## Education

**CONFIRMED** — Graduated from **Beijing Fashion Institute** (北京服装学院 / Beijing Institute of Fashion Technology, BIFT).

Source: ACG Gateway artist bio (Japanese): 「中国人イラストレーター、湖南省出身、北京服装学院卒業。」
("Chinese illustrator, from Hunan Province, graduated from Beijing Fashion Institute.")

Graduation year not stated. Given approximate age of 26 as of 2026, likely graduated c. 2021–2023.

Note: Beijing Fashion Institute is a specialist design and fashion university in Beijing, not a traditional fine arts academy. Her background is in illustration/design, not classical painting. This explains the illustrator-first framing of her early career.

## Career Practice

- **Daily watercolor diary series** ("diary"): Started 2020. Ongoing series of daily watercolor sketches posted to social media. The full 2020 diary was posted to Twitter January 2021.
- **Aspiration noted in ACG bio**: "aspires to become a costume photographer" — secondary creative direction alongside painting.
- **Primary medium**: Watercolor. Subjects include seasonal imagery, urban streetscapes, everyday domestic scenes, cats, architectural corners.
