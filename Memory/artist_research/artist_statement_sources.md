### Verbatim Japanese text

> 中国人イラストレーター、湖南省出身、北京服装学院卒業。水彩画を得意とし、将来はイラストを描くコスチュームカメラマンになることを目指す。『defined Definition 02: A Documented Journey』や、きのこ王国とのオフライングループ展の制作に携わり、2021年10月に初のソロイラストレーション集『Colour Diary』を刊行。また2020年から毎日の水彩画を描き、それらは「diary」という名称で続けている。作品は、まるで北国を旅する列車のように、春夏秋冬に満ちあふれ、それらは暖かい日差しやそよ風を感じさせ、見る者に見慣れた街角や公園を散策するような感覚を覚えさせる。赤い壁、路地、プール、緑の池…ごく普通の日常を感動的なシーンに。

### English translation (working)

Chinese illustrator, from Hunan Province, graduated from Beijing Fashion Institute. Specializes in watercolor, and aspires to become a costume photographer who also draws illustrations. Has participated in *defined Definition 02: A Documented Journey* and offline group exhibitions with Kinoko Kingdom (きのこ王国). In October 2021, published first solo illustration collection *Colour Diary*. Since 2020, has painted daily watercolors, continuing a series called "diary." Works are like a train traveling through the north — filled with all four seasons, conveying warm sunlight and a gentle breeze, giving the viewer the sensation of strolling through familiar street corners and parks. Red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds… transforming the utterly ordinary everyday into moving scenes.

### Key facts confirmed

- Origin: Hunan Province, China
- Education: Beijing Fashion Institute (北京服装学院), illustration/design
- Medium: watercolor
- Secondary aspiration: "costume photographer who draws illustrations" (コスチュームカメラマン) — this is specific and unusual; may reflect a genuine dual-career interest or a translation artifact
- Publications: *Colour Diary* (Oct 2021); contribution to *defined Definition 02*
- Group shows: Kinoko Kingdom (China); Tide from China Part1 (Tokyo, Feb 2023 — first Japan show)
- Daily practice: watercolor diary series called "diary", begun 2020
- Subject: ordinary daily life, urban spaces, seasons, streets, parks

### Notes on the poetic description

The last two sentences ("Works are like a train traveling through the north…") are unusual in exhibition bio format — they read like artist-written copy, not journalist description. The level of specificity (列車, 北国, 赤い壁, 路地, プール, 緑の池) and the lyrical register suggest this passage is closest to the artist's own voice. It is the single best candidate for an artist statement seed.
