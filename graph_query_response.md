## Show Concept:

**"The Last Alley: Beijing Before Erasure"**

Documentary approaches across media (painting, photography, printmaking) examining disappearing hutong neighborhoods and vernacular architecture. All work shares preservation impulse, muted palettes, and empathetic distance. Mix of established (Liu Xiaodong) and emerging voices creates market accessibility.

---

## Why This Works:
- **All Beijing artists** (or China-based) = logistically feasible, shared cultural context
- **Medium diversity** prevents redundancy: 3 painters + 1 photographer + 1 printmaker
- **Range of career stages**: Liu Xiaodong (established) anchors newer practitioners
- **Coherent collector appeal**: Documentary realism attracts overlapping buyer demographics
- **Strong thematic through-line**: Urban transformation, preservation, Chinese domestic spaces
