# Publishing — When Someone Else Makes the Book

**Prepared 2026-09-06 for GEGYjiji (Nin), Tokyo.**
Research only. No advice, no strategy — figures and their sources.

**Companion to [`book_economics_2026-09-05.md`](book_economics_2026-09-05.md)**, which covers self-publishing exhaustively (printing, freight, consignment, fairs, pricing, break-even). **Nothing in that report is repeated here.**

**Scope — one narrow thing.** A *publisher* takes on a book of her paintings, **carries the cost and the print run**, distributes it nationally, and pays her a **royalty (印税 / 著作物利用料)**. That is 商業出版. Not self-publishing. Not being paid to illustrate someone else's book. Not a hybrid service where she pays.

**Confidence tags:** **DIRECT** = read off an official page or contract · **INFERRED** = derived by a stated method · **UNVERIFIED** = could not confirm · **ANECDOTE** = single self-report, n=1, source named.

**Consumption tax:** Japanese royalties are computed on **本体価格 (ex-tax cover price)** — established in §2.1, and used throughout. Books carry 10% consumption tax, so 本体 = 税込 ÷ 1.1.

## 1. The boundary — three different things get called "publishing"

Establishing this first, because two of the three Japanese art-publisher routes that surface in a search are **not** the thing in scope, and one of them is designed to look like it is.

| Model | Who pays production | What she receives | Distribution | In scope here? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **商業出版** (trade) | **Publisher** | **印税, ~3–10% of 本体価格** | National, via 取次 → bookshops | ✅ **This report** |
| **自費出版 / hybrid** | **Artist** | 40–60% of cover, but after paying to print | Varies; sometimes real bookshop distribution | ❌ — but §1.3, because it is the trap |
| **私家版** (self-published) | Artist | ~¥2,750–4,184/copy margin | Her own hands: BASE, fairs, consignment | ❌ — companion report |

## 3. The money

### 3.1 The 8–10% figure — what the evidence actually supports

The 8–10% number circulating in this project was **unsourced**. It is now **partly supported and materially qualified**. Here is the evidence, best first.

#### The authoritative anchor: the industry model contract fixes no rate at all

The **日本書籍出版協会 (Japan Book Publishers Association) 2015 出版権設定契約書ヒナ型1** is the standard-form publishing contract used across the Japanese trade. Its royalty appendix is **blank**. Verbatim from the contract PDF ([jbpa.or.jp/pdf/publication/hinagata2015-1.pdf](https://www.jbpa.or.jp/pdf/publication/hinagata2015-1.pdf), text extracted locally — **DIRECT**):

> （別掲）著作物利用料等について
> 本出版物について
>  **実売部数１部ごとに** ＿＿
>  **保証部数** ＿＿＿＿ 部
>  **保証金額** ＿＿＿＿ 円
> 本出版物について
>  **発行部数１部ごとに** ＿＿

And from the association's own commentary ([hinagata1kaisetsu.pdf](https://www.jbpa.or.jp/pdf/publication/hinagata1kaisetsu.pdf), **DIRECT**), which fills the same blanks with a worked *記載例*:

> 実売部数１部ごとに **本体価格（税別）の○％** ⇒ この欄は、実売部数印税方式を採用している場合に使用します。
> 発行部数１部ごとに **本体価格（税別）の○％** ⇒ この欄は、発行部数印税方式を採用している場合に使用します。
> **保証金の支払いについて：刊行月の末日に一括して指定口座に振り込む。**
> **保証分を超えた分の支払いについて：毎年○月及び○月末締めで、出荷冊数の○割を実売部数とし、翌月末日に振り込む。**
> （発行部数方式の場合）**発行の都度、○日以内に指定口座に振り込む。**
> 電子出版について：１ダウンロードごとに、希望配信価格×○％（みなし価格方式）／配信事業者からの入金の○％（レベニューシェア方式）

**Five DIRECT findings from that, all of which matter more than the percentage:**

1. **The base is 本体価格（税別）** — the ex-tax cover price. Not the tax-inclusive price, not net receipts.
2. **There are two systems.** 発行部数印税 pays on **copies printed**, whether or not they sell, within a set number of days of each printing. 実売部数印税 pays on **copies sold**. Which one she gets is the single biggest variable after the rate itself.
3. **The Japanese analogue of an advance is 保証部数 / 保証金額** — a guaranteed minimum number of copies and a guaranteed sum, **paid in one lump at the end of the publication month**. Not on signature.
4. Under 実売 accounting, "sold" is not measured. It is **defined**: 「出荷冊数の○割を実売部数とし」 — a stated fraction of *shipped* copies is *deemed* sold. Settlement is **twice a year**.
5. **The percentage is a blank.** The trade association that publishes the standard contract **does not publish a standard rate.** Anyone quoting a fixed Japanese royalty rate as an industry figure is quoting a custom, not a rule.

#### The best art-book-specific evidence: a working Tokyo illustrator's own model

**タケウマ / studio_takeuma** — the same illustrator the companion report identifies as the closest comparable to Nin (Tokyo, ~27K Instagram, self-publishes art books). In 「私家版画集の流通について 05」 he sets out the cover-price split he was taught by publishers. Verbatim ([note.com/studio_takeuma/n/n569c579f74c0](https://note.com/studio_takeuma/n/n569c579f74c0), **DIRECT**):

> **商業出版：著者（印税）：10 / 制作コスト（デザイン費+印刷製本代）：20 / 取次：10 / 書店：20 / 出版社：40**
> 私家版 Ａ 直卸し：著者：10 / 制作コスト：**55** / 書店：35
> 私家版 Ｂ 流通委託：著者：10 / 制作コスト：**45** / ディストリビューター：10 / 書店：35

And the qualification that changes the whole picture for a full-colour art book:

> 「**著者+制作コストの値は各社とも35程度**だろうと教わりました。**制作コストを捻出するため著者 3 とかもあります。**」
> 「関係業者が絞られる分、制作コストを上乗せできることが私家版のメリットです。」

**Read that carefully.** Author royalty and production cost together are capped at roughly **35 points of cover price**, and they compete for the same money. A monochrome text book leaves room for a 10% royalty. **A 128-page full-colour hardcover eats the production budget — and the author's share is what gets cut. He says so explicitly: 著者 3.**

⚠️ **Confidence: DIRECT read of a named professional's published account, but it is one practitioner's model, not audited data.** He is a working illustrator with commercially published books, which makes it the best art-book-specific evidence found — and still n=1.

> ### **Verdict on 8–10%**
> **10% is the ceiling, and it is the figure the trade quotes for a normal book.** For a **full-colour art book by a first-time author**, the evidence points **lower**: the production cost and the author's share draw on the same ~35 points, and the only art-book-specific source found names **3%** as a real outcome when production is expensive. **A defensible planning band is 5–8%, with 3% possible and 10% optimistic.**
> **The figure is now sourced. It is also weaker than it looked.**

> ### **The trade in one line**
> **300 books she sells herself ≈ 5,000–16,000 books a publisher sells.**
> **But 300 books she sells herself is years of fairs, shipping, storage and admin — the companion report's measured median is 20–25 copies per one-day fair. 5,000 books a publisher sells costs her nothing after delivery.**
> **That is the whole decision. It is a labour question wearing a money question's clothes.**

### 3.4 Advance vs royalty

**There is no evidence of an on-signature advance convention in Japanese trade publishing.** What the model contract provides instead is **保証金額** — a guaranteed sum, **paid in one lump at the end of the publication month** (`刊行月の末日に一括して指定口座に振り込む`, DIRECT, §3.1). Money arrives **when the book comes out**, not when the deal is signed.

Under the **発行部数印税方式** there is no advance at all in the Western sense — but there is something better: she is paid on **every copy printed**, `発行の都度、○日以内`, whether the shop sells it or not. **DIRECT.**

🔴 **UNVERIFIED:** whether Japanese art publishers actually offer 保証部数 to first-time artists, and at what level. The mechanism is in the standard contract; **no observed instance of it being used for an art book was found.**

### 3.5 🔴 GAP — print runs for a first art book

**No figure could be sourced.** What was tried: MdN's press-release archive and product page for 東京店構え (no 部数 stated, **DIRECT verified absence**); a title search on books.mdn.co.jp for 重版 / 万部 announcements (the search returned unfiltered new releases); Japanese search for 初版部数 相場, which returned only definitional pages and one vague trade statement — 「初版の部数は、数百部から数千、数万部までさまざまです」 ([sankeisha.com](https://www.sankeisha.com/iroha/printedition), **DIRECT but useless as a figure**).

**The only quantified anchors that exist:**

| Anchor | Figure | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Urbanowicz **series** total, Japan | **「has sold over 100,000 copies in Japan alone」** | **DIRECT read of his own site** — [mateuszurbanowicz.com, 2026-02-18](https://mateuszurbanowicz.com/2026/02/18/exhibition-at-azabudai-hills-tokyo/) · ⚠️ **promotional self-report, unaudited, series not per-title** |

## 4. How a book actually gets acquired

Four verified cases, each with a different trigger. **No folklore is included; where a mechanism is only inferred, it says so.**

### 4.1 Case 1 — Urbanowicz / MdN: SNS **plus** mass media **plus** a professional credit

MdN's own book page and press release state the chain verbatim ([books.mdn.co.jp/release/58382/](https://books.mdn.co.jp/release/58382/) and [/books/3217303025/](https://books.mdn.co.jp/books/3217303025/), **DIRECT**):

> 「**2016年にインターネット上で発表されて話題となり、新聞やテレビなどでも取り上げられた**人気イラスト企画が待望の書籍化！」

**And the part usually left out of the telling:** the same page notes he worked on background art at an animation studio under 新海誠 including **『君の名は。』**. **DIRECT.**

| Fact | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher | **MdN** | DIRECT |
| Publication | **2018-04-25**, B5変型判, オールカラー, **160pp**, ¥2,530 税込 (¥2,300 本体), ISBN 978-4-8443-6734-5 | DIRECT |
| Content | 10 previously published illustrations + **40 newly commissioned** = 50 works, plus making-of, bird's-eye diagrams, building histories | DIRECT |
| Editor | サイドランチ (an outside editorial studio) | DIRECT |

**Three things this case establishes and one thing it does not.** It establishes that **MdN buys a project, not a portfolio** (a named series with a subject); that **most of the book was made after the deal** — 40 of 50 works were newly drawn, so a finished book was *not* what he brought; and that an **outside editorial studio packaged it**. It does **not** establish that Instagram alone is sufficient: the chain ran online → newspapers and TV → book, and he carried a feature-film credit.

### 4.2 Case 2 — 撫子凛 / マール社: an editor emailed after a visible milestone

First-person account ([note.com/nadeshicorin/n/n320c16c84d56](https://note.com/nadeshicorin/n/n320c16c84d56), **DIRECT read; ANECDOTE n=1**). She posted daily, and on hitting a self-set goal tweeted:

> 「浮世絵で見つけた美男美女を趣味で書いている #お江戸スタイルブック 当初の目標だった**100体突破しました**！」

> 「**出版社の編集の方からメールをいただいたのです。**」

Result: 『イラストでわかる お江戸ファッション図鑑』, マール社, **2021-01-07**, A5判, **144pp**, ¥1,870 税込, ISBN 978-4-8373-0916-1 — **DIRECT** from [maar.com](https://www.maar.com/shop/comic/pose/isbn9784837309161). She has since published a second title with them (『小袖雛形ファッションブック』, ¥1,980).

> ⚠️ **Correction, stated because the brief warned about exactly this.** It is tempting to call this "her first book." **It was not.** Her author bio on Maar's own page names a prior 2020 title, 『おとなの教養ぬりえ&なぞり描き 歌舞伎』. **DIRECT.** The acquisition mechanism is verified; the "debut" framing is not, and is not claimed here.

**What is genuinely transferable:** the trigger was **a named, hashtagged series with a public count** — a body of work legible as a project, announced at a milestone. Not a follower number. She names her own causes as 「根気と『江戸が好き』と言う気持ち」 — persistence and a specific subject love.

### 4.3 Case 3 — あべとしゆき / 芸術新聞社: the watercolour route, which looks nothing like the others

The genre-closest case, and the most sobering. From the publisher's own author bio ([gei-shin.co.jp/books/books-6935/](https://gei-shin.co.jp/books/books-6935/), **DIRECT**):

- Born 1959, Sakata, Yamagata. Career in newspaper design and art teaching. **Turned professional watercolourist in 2008.**
- Prizes: **2010 内藤秀因記念水彩画展 大賞** · **2011 日本水彩展 上野の森美術館賞**
- Member of **日本美術家連盟** and **JWS 日本透明水彩会**
- **Wrote multiple watercolour technique books first**
- Then, **2023-09-25**: 『あべとしゆき水彩画集』 — B5横, 176pp, **192 self-selected works**, ¥3,300 税込

**The route is: competition prizes → society membership → technique books → 画集.** The 画集 came **fifteen years** into a professional practice and **after** the technique books had proved a readership. **DIRECT.**

💡 **This is directly connected to her existing pipeline.** 日本水彩展 — the exhibition that produced one of his two named prizes — is already an opportunity record in this system (第113回 日本水彩展).

### 4.4 Case 4 — the group route: a society anthology from a commercial publisher

『日本を旅する水彩風景スケッチ』, **グラフィック社**, 2012-03, B5変, 並製, **200pp**, ¥2,640 税込, ISBN 978-4-7661-2339-5 — edited by **日本水彩画会**, **87 contributing artists**, 180+ works, published for the society's **100th anniversary**. **DIRECT** — [graphicsha.co.jp](https://www.graphicsha.co.jp/detail.html?p=28951).

**A real, verified fifth door:** a commercial art publisher will carry a book where the *society* is the author and the artists are contributors. No individual proposal required — membership and selection are the qualification. **What a contributor is paid in such a book is UNVERIFIED.**

### 4.5 Does anyone scout SNS? Yes — but not by follower count

| Evidence | What it says | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 撫子凛 (§4.2) | An editor cold-emailed after a milestone tweet | **ANECDOTE n=1, named, first-person** |
| MdN's own copy (§4.1) | Online attention → newspapers/TV → book | **DIRECT** |
| [本づくり研究所, 2018-01-23](https://lab.p-press.jp/report/report44.html) | 「投稿のたびに**何千何万と『いいね』『リツイート』されていれば、出版社にとっては大きな安心材料になります**」 | **DIRECT read, secondary, 2018, and about text/photo creators not painters.** The report names no discovery mechanism |
| マール社 (§2.1) | Inbound works: many of their authors had never made a book | **DIRECT** |

> **No source found anywhere states a follower threshold.** Every named mechanism is about a *body of work legible as a project* — a series, a milestone, a subject — plus, in two of three cases, corroboration outside SNS (mass media, or prior commercial credits).

### 4.6 Do publishers respond to a finished self-published book?

🔴 **GAP — no direct evidence either way.** Not one account was found of a Japanese publisher acquiring a book because they saw the artist's self-published edition.

**Adjacent facts that bear on it, without resolving it:**
- マール社 explicitly warns that **submitted materials are not returned** — 「ご応募いただいた原稿、企画書、資料等はご返却できません」 (DIRECT). Posting a physical book to a publisher means losing that copy.
- 青幻舎 accepts **data only, 10MB max**, and destroys it after review (DIRECT). There is no channel for a physical object.
- Per the companion report (§3.3, DIRECT from [tokyoartbookfair.com](https://tokyoartbookfair.com/application-addition/)), TABF's stated selection priority is 「**定期的に出版物を刊行している方**」— people who publish regularly. A self-published record is treated as a qualification *by the fair*, whose audience includes editors. **That is suggestive of the mechanism and is not proof of it.**

### 4.7 Is an agent involved in Japan? Essentially no

| Body | What it actually is | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Japan UNI Agency** (est. 1967, 「the longest-established copyright agency in Japan」) | **Rights agency between publishers** — 「World Works to Japan」 / 「Japanese Works to the World」. **Not author-side representation to domestic publishers.** | **DIRECT** — [japanuni.co.jp](https://japanuni.co.jp/en/) |
| **CWC / Cross World Connections** (Tokyo) | Commercial **illustration agency** — 「国籍を超えた個性あるイラストレーターたちのプロモーションやマネージメント、ライセンスを行っています」. Advertising, licensing, merchandising. Nothing on its About page describes book acquisition | **DIRECT** — [cwctokyo.com/about](https://www.cwctokyo.com/about) · ⚠️ **absence of a claim is not proof they don't do it** |

**INFERRED conclusion:** Japan has rights agencies and illustration agencies; it does not have the Anglo-American author's agent who sells a book proposal to a publisher on commission. **The three published submission doors in §2 exist precisely because there is no agent layer.** She would approach publishers herself.

**Quoted verbatim, the two clearest statements of what an international art publisher wants:**

**Thames & Hudson** ([thamesandhudson.com/pages/getting-published](https://thamesandhudson.com/pages/getting-published), **DIRECT**):
> "We are always open to suggestions and submissions from authors, photographers, designers and illustrators with new proposals for books."
> — but: **"we... decline portfolio submissions from illustrators/photographers offering services."**

**Hoxton Mini Press** ([hoxtonminipress.com/pages/submissions](https://www.hoxtonminipress.com/pages/submissions), **DIRECT**):
> "We are hugely interested in seeing your work; it's our lifeblood."
> Wanted: urban stories and cities (London **and worldwide**), **architecture, particularly urban**, nature meeting the urban, social history, art and design.
> **"We are obsessed by high quality imagery."**
> Every project must have "appeal to a wider audience (through subject matter, quirk or pure emotional force)".

> ### **The single most important pattern in this section**
> **Every publisher asks for a *book idea*, and several explicitly refuse a *portfolio***. T&H and gestalten both state, in near-identical words, that they do not take portfolio submissions from illustrators offering services. **Beautiful paintings are not a proposal. "A book about X, for readers Y, competing with Z, and here are 6 images" is a proposal.**
> The Urbanowicz case (§4.1) is the same fact from the other side: what MdN bought was 「東京店構え」— *Tokyo storefronts* — a subject with a boundary, not "watercolours by Mateusz."

## 7. The trade, honestly stated — quantified from the standard contract

This section uses the **JBPA 2015 出版権設定契約書ヒナ型1** ([contract](https://www.jbpa.or.jp/pdf/publication/hinagata2015-1.pdf) / [commentary](https://www.jbpa.or.jp/pdf/publication/hinagata1kaisetsu.pdf)) — the form the Japanese trade actually uses. **All quotations DIRECT.** Individual contracts vary; this is the default she would be negotiating away from.

### 7.1 What she gives up — clause by clause

| Clause | Verbatim | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **第1条（2）** | 「乙は、本著作物に関し、**日本を含むすべての国と地域において**、…行為を行う権利を**専有**する」 | **Exclusive, worldwide.** Print, packaged electronic, and transmission rights. Not a Japan-only licence. |
| ⭐ **第8条（2）** | 「乙は、…の場合の**価格、造本、製作部数、増刷の時期、宣伝方法およびその他の販売方法**、ならびに…**価格、宣伝方法、配信方法および利用条件等を決定する**」 | **The publisher decides the object.** Price. **造本** — format, binding, paper, the physical book. Print run. Whether there is a reprint. Advertising. How it is sold. For an artist whose subject *is* surface and atmosphere, **this is the expensive clause, not the royalty.** |
| **第7条** | 「乙は、本著作物の内容・表現または**書名・題号等に変更**を加える必要が生じた場合には、**あらかじめ著作者の承諾**を得なければならない」 | She keeps a veto on **content and title**. She has **no veto on the object**. The line falls exactly between §7 and §8(2). |
| ⭐ **第3条（1）** | 「甲は、本契約の有効期間中、本著作物の全部または一部と**同一もしくは明らかに類似すると認められる内容**の著作物…について、…出版利用を、**自ら行わず、かつ第三者をして行わせない**」 | **A non-compete on her own work.** During the term she cannot self-publish, or let anyone else publish, the same *or clearly similar* material. **This directly forecloses the zine practice she already has.** |
| ⭐ **第3条（2）** | 「甲が本著作物の全部または一部を、**甲自らのホームページ（ブログ、メールマガジン等を含む）**において利用しようとする場合には、甲は**事前に乙に通知し、その同意を得なければならない**」 | Posting the work herself requires **prior notice and the publisher's consent**. ⚠️ **The model text names homepage, blog and mail magazine. It does not name SNS or Instagram.** Whether 「等」 reaches Instagram is exactly the sentence to negotiate — **for a painter whose practice is a six-year daily Instagram diary, this is the clause that decides whether the deal is livable.** |
| **第5条（1）** | 「甲は、本契約の有効期間中のみならず**終了後であっても**、本出版物の**版面**を利用した印刷物の出版または本出版物の**電子データ**…の利用を、乙の事前の書面による承諾なく行わず」 | **Permanent.** Even after the contract ends she cannot reprint the publisher's typeset edition or reuse the production files without written consent. Her copyright is untouched (第5条2) — the *edition* is not hers. |
| **第12条** | 「本契約の有効期間は、契約の日から満＿ヵ年…期間満了の**３ヵ月前**までに…終了する旨の通告がないときは、**自動的に継続**され」 | Fixed term in years, **auto-renewing** unless one side gives three months' written notice. |
| **第13条（1）** | 「乙は、…期間満了による終了後も、著作物利用料の支払いを条件として、**本出版物の在庫に限り販売することができる**」 | After termination the publisher keeps selling remaining stock (paying royalties on it). |
| **第4条（2）** | 「乙が、本出版物を**納本、贈呈、批評、宣伝、販売促進、業務**等に利用する場合（＿部を上限とする）…**著作物利用料が免除される**」 | Review, publicity and promotional copies pay her **nothing**. The cap is a blank — **fill it in.** |
| **第9条（1）** | 「初版第一刷の際に＿部、増刷のつど＿部を甲に**贈呈**する」 | Free author copies, count negotiable. |
| **第9条（2）** (from the 記載例) | 「甲が寄贈等のために…**直接購入**する場合、乙は、**本体価格の＿％**で提供する」 | Her own author-purchase discount is a **blank**. This is the clause that determines whether she can sell copies at her own exhibitions — **negotiate it.** |
| **第6条** | She delegates management of copying and lending rights to the publisher | Reprographic and library-lending income flows through them. |

### 7.2 What she gets — and the honest size of it

| What a publisher gives | Quantified where possible |
|---|---|
| **No outlay** | Against **¥320,000–2.7M** of print cost she would otherwise carry (companion §1.1). **This is the largest single number in either report.** |
| **National bookshop distribution** | マール社: 「**できあがった本は全国書店で発売されます**」 (DIRECT). Compare the companion's finding: **none** of her three target indie shops publishes consignment terms at all, and consignment only works at all if landed unit cost is under ~¥1,500 (companion §6.2). |
| **The 取次 machine** | She never touches 納品書, 掛率 negotiation, 3–6-month settlement lags, returns, or storage. Per the companion §2.4, direct-to-shop trade at 70掛 nets her the same share a whole publisher nets via 取次 — but she does all the work. |
| **Credibility** | An ISBN, a publisher's name, a book that libraries carry (第4条(2) names 納本 — legal deposit at the 国会図書館). Real and unquantifiable. |
| **Scale that self-publishing cannot reach** | Urbanowicz's series: **100,000+ copies in Japan** (self-reported, §3.5). The companion's measured self-publishing median: **20–25 copies per fair.** |
| **Rights beyond Japan** | 第1条(2) is worldwide — the flip side of the exclusivity: the publisher is *obliged* to have the machinery to exploit it. |
| **Editorial packaging** | Urbanowicz's book was assembled by an outside editorial studio, サイドランチ (DIRECT). Someone else does the sequencing, captioning, translation and layout. |

> ### **The trade, in the plainest terms the evidence supports**
> **They pay for everything and take the object.** She keeps her copyright and a veto on content; she loses the decision about paper, binding, size, price, print run and whether it ever reprints. She trades **¥3,635 a copy on 300 copies** for **¥69–470 a copy on a number neither she nor this report can predict**, and she trades **years of her own labour** for **none**.
> **On money alone, self-publishing wins until roughly 5,000–16,000 publisher-sold copies. On labour, the publisher wins immediately. Neither report can tell her which of those she is short of.**
