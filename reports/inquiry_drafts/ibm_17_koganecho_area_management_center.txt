Subject: Exhibition Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Artist (Tokyo)

Dear Koganecho Area Management Center,

I am writing because your program's commitment to site-responsive practice and the revitalization of Yokohama's everyday urban fabric feels directly connected to the work I make: watercolor paintings that move through the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds — and try to return those scenes to the viewer as something quietly moving. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice called "diary," which became the foundation for my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and I believe that accumulative, place-attentive way of working is well suited to the kind of community-embedded, neighborhood-rooted context Koganecho fosters. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province, with exhibition experience in Japan most recently through Tide from China Part 1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023, and my ongoing work can be followed at Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/). I would be grateful to learn more about your current programs and residency cycles, and whether there may be a suitable point of entry for a proposal. Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

GEGYjiji
