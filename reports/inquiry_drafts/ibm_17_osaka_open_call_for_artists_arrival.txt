Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji (Watercolor / Tokyo-based)

Hello ARRIVAL team,

Your Osaka open call caught my attention because the work you platform tends to sit with quiet, atmospheric pieces that find something resonant in the unremarkable — which is exactly the territory my watercolor practice occupies. Since 2020 I have kept a daily watercolor diary, painting the things close at hand: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner, transforming the utterly ordinary into something that moves. This ongoing series, along with my first solo illustration collection Colour Diary (October 2021), forms the core of what I would bring to this submission. My work is visible at Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/), where I share the diary pieces regularly. I would be glad to send selected works, artist statement, or any materials your submission process requires — please let me know what you need and I will follow your guidelines.

GEGYjiji
