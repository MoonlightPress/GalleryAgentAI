Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Illustration

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair team,

Tokyo Art Book Fair's reputation for bringing together independent artists, zines, and artist books at MOT makes it one of the few fairs in Japan where a self-published illustration work genuinely belongs. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and I would like to inquire about the exhibitor application process for the 2026 fair. My practice centers on a daily watercolor series called "diary," which I have maintained since 2020 and which formed the basis of my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021 — work that moves through ordinary scenes, red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, and tries to make something emotionally resonant from the unremarkable everyday. You can see the ongoing work at instagram.com/gegyjiji. I would be grateful to know the application deadline, any booth or table fees, and where to submit — and am happy to provide more information about my work if helpful.

GEGYjiji
