Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Illustration

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair Team,

Tokyo Art Book Fair's focus on artist-made books and independent publishing at MOT makes it one of the few fairs in Japan where a publication like Colour Diary — my first solo illustration collection, which grew directly from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020 — would sit comfortably among its peers. My work moves through the ordinary: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the particular warmth of sunlight in a familiar street, transformed into what I hope are emotionally resonant scenes. The "diary" series that underlies Colour Diary is still ongoing, and the seriality of that daily observation feels well-suited to the artist book context your fair centers. I would be grateful to learn whether applications for 2026 exhibitor booths are currently open, and where I can find the submission guidelines and any relevant deadlines or fees. A selection of recent work is available on my Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, and I am happy to send any additional materials you require.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
