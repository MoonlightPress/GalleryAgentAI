Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo

Dear Art Vancouver 2026 Team,

I am writing to inquire about the exhibitor application process for Art Vancouver 2026, which I understand welcomes contemporary art and design from both Canadian and international artists. My name is GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work focuses on the ordinary made vivid — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street — transforming everyday scenes into emotionally resonant paintings. In October 2021 I published my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, which grew directly from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020, and I made my first Japan exhibition in February 2023 as part of Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku. My ongoing work can be seen on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/ where I share new watercolors daily. Could you please share details on the application process, any relevant deadlines, and booth or participation fees?

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

GEGYjiji
