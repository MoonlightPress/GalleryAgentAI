Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Illustration

Dear ART SG 2026 Team,

I am writing to inquire about exhibitor opportunities at ART SG 2026, which I understand focuses on contemporary Asian art and provides a platform for emerging voices across the region — an context I feel is well-suited to my work as a Beijing-born, Tokyo-based watercolor illustrator. My practice centers on quiet urban observation, domestic light, and cross-cultural memory, developed through a daily watercolor diary I have maintained since 2020. My first solo collection, Colour Diary, grew directly from that practice and has been received warmly within illustration communities in Japan and online. Most recently, I participated in Tide from China Part 1, a group exhibition in Harajuku featuring six Chinese illustrators working in Japan, which gave me a first sense of how this work translates into a gallery context. I would be grateful to learn about the application process, relevant deadlines, and any fees, and you are welcome to view my portfolio at instagram.com/gegyjiji.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

GEGYjiji
