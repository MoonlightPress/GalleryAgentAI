Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — ART SG 2026 / GEGYjiji (Watercolor, Tokyo)

Dear ART SG 2026 Team,

ART SG's positioning as a platform for contemporary Asian voices — including work from Chinese artists based across the region — makes it a context I have been following with genuine interest. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province, and my practice centers on the ordinary made moving: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street. This daily observational work grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and continues as an ongoing painted diary series I have maintained without interruption since 2020. I would be grateful to understand whether ART SG 2026 accepts applications from independent artists or whether participation is structured through gallery representation, and if so, what the submission process and relevant deadlines look like. A selection of my work is visible on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/ — around 90,000 followers — and I am happy to provide additional materials on request.

GEGYjiji
