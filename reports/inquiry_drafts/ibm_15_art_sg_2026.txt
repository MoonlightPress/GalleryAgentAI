Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — ART SG 2026 / GEGYjiji (Watercolor, Tokyo)

Dear ART SG 2026 Team,

I am writing to ask about the exhibitor application process for ART SG 2026, as I understand Asia Art Center is presenting the fair and I would like to explore whether participation is available to independent artists. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo — my work moves through ordinary scenes that tend to go unnoticed: red walls, alleyways, pools, the particular quality of light in a familiar street — and transforms them into something quietly moving. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, grew directly from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020, and recent work can be seen on Instagram at @gegyjiji. As a Chinese artist working in Japan, ART SG feels like a meaningful context in which to bring this work to a wider audience across the region. I would be grateful to know how applications are structured, what the relevant deadlines are, and whether there is a format suited to a solo artist at this stage.

Warm regards,
GEGYjiji
