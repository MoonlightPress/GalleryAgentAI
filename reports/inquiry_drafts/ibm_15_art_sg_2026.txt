Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — ART SG 2026 / GEGYjiji (Watercolor / Tokyo)

Dear ART SG 2026 Team,

I am writing to ask about the exhibitor application process for ART SG 2026, as I understand the fair positions itself as a platform for contemporary Asian art with strong representation of Chinese and diaspora voices. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work moves through the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, green ponds, the particular light of a familiar street — finding in those everyday scenes something that feels at once specific and quietly universal. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, grew directly from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020, and the series continues. My work can be seen at Instagram @gegyjiji, and I made my first Japan exhibition appearance in February 2023 as part of Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku, Tokyo. I would be grateful for any information on how to apply, relevant deadlines, and booth or participation formats available to independent artists.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
