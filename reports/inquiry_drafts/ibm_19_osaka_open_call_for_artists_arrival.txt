Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji (Watercolor / Tokyo-based)

Dear ARRIVAL Team,

I came across your Osaka open call and was drawn to the structured, curated approach ARRIVAL takes to introducing artists — it feels like a considered context for work that asks the viewer to slow down. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my practice centers on the utterly ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner — scenes most people walk past, which I try to make into something moving. This work grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and continues through an ongoing daily watercolor series I have kept since 2020. A fuller picture of the work is on Instagram at @gegyjiji. I would be glad to share image files, a statement, or any additional materials your selection process requires — please let me know what the submission asks for and I will send everything promptly.

GEGYjiji
