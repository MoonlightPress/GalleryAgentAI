Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Illustration

Dear ARRIVAL team,

I came across your Osaka open call and was drawn to the way ARRIVAL platforms intimate, atmosphere-driven work — a sensibility that aligns closely with my own practice. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work focuses on quiet urban observation, interior light, and the small details of daily life seen through a cross-cultural lens. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, grew from a daily watercolor diary practice I have maintained since 2020, and my work has been shown in Tokyo as part of the group exhibition Tide from China at ACG_Labo, Harajuku, in February 2023. You can view my portfolio and recent work on Instagram at instagram.com/gegyjiji. I would be grateful for the chance to learn more about the submission requirements and would welcome the opportunity to participate.

GEGYjiji
