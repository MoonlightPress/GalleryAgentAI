Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Illustrator

Dear ARRIVAL team,

I came across your Osaka open call and was drawn to the kind of work you platform — intimate, atmospheric, and grounded in the texture of everyday life, which speaks directly to my own practice. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have painted daily watercolors — a series called "diary" — that find their subject in the utterly ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner. This daily practice grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and continues to shape everything I make. My work is less about documentation than about the feeling of walking through a place you half-remember — each season arriving the way it does on a slow train through the north. You can find a full view of my ongoing work on Instagram at @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/), and I would be glad to share a portfolio or answer any questions about the submission process.

GEGYjiji
