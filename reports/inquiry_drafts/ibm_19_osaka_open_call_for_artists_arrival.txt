Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji (Watercolor / Illustration)

Dear ARRIVAL team,

I came across your Osaka open call and was drawn to ARRIVAL's focus on bringing together artists across contexts and geographies — it feels well-suited to the kind of work I make. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my practice centers on finding emotional resonance in the utterly ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor series called "diary," and this sustained observation of everyday life became the foundation of my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work and full portfolio can be found on Instagram at @gegyjiji. I would be glad to submit materials and learn more about your submission requirements and any associated fees.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

GEGYjiji
