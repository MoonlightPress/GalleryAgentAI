Subject: Exhibition Proposal for Gallery 456 Open Call 2026 , GEGYjiji, Watercolor Illustrator

Dear Gallery 456 Team,

Gallery 456's open call for artists with Chinese and Asian heritage work feels like the right context for what I make. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor illustrator based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province. My work begins in ordinary places: the particular light on a platform in the morning, or the stillness of a café before it fills. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice called "diary," which grew into my first solo collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My first Japan exhibition was in February 2023, as part of Tide from China Part 1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku, Tokyo, a group show with five other Chinese illustrators. You can see my ongoing work at Instagram @gegyjiji: https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/

I would be glad to share more about available works, sizes, and scheduling at your convenience.

GEGYjiji
