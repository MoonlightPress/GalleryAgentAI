Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — Art Vancouver 2026

Dear Art Vancouver team,

Art Vancouver's record of bringing international contemporary artists alongside Canadian voices — and its reach into Vancouver's culturally diverse audience — makes it a context I'd like to explore seriously. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work centers on the ordinary scenes that pass unnoticed: red walls, alleyways, pools, the light in a familiar room, the feeling of walking through a place you half-remember. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice called "diary," which became the foundation of my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work is currently visible on Instagram at @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/), where I post from this ongoing daily practice. I would be grateful to learn whether exhibitor applications for 2026 are still open, what the submission process involves, and whether my work might be a considered fit for the fair.

GEGYjiji
