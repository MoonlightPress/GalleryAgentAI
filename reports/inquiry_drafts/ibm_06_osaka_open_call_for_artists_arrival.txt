Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Illustrator

Dear ARRIVAL team,

I am writing to submit my work for your Osaka open call. My practice centers on the ordinary scenes most people pass without pausing — red walls, alleyways, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar park — and finding within them something that feels, I hope, quietly moving. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor series called "diary," and this sustained attention to everyday life was the direct source for my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. I made my first exhibition in Japan as part of Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023, and I am now looking to bring my work to Osaka. You can see my ongoing practice at Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/), where I post daily.

I would welcome any information on submission requirements, fees, and deadline, and am happy to send selected works or any additional materials you need.

GEGYjiji
