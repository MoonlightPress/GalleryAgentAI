Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo Gendai 2026

Dear Tokyo Gendai Team,

I am writing to ask about the process for individual artists or small exhibitors to participate in Tokyo Gendai 2026, as I understand gallery applications are currently open for the September edition. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work finds its subjects in the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, the light in a familiar park — and tries to make those scenes feel like somewhere the viewer half-remembers. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, grew directly from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020, and I made my first Tokyo exhibition as part of the group show Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in 2023. My work can be seen on Instagram at @gegyjiji. I would be grateful for any information on how independent artists may apply, and whether the format of the fair accommodates smaller presentations.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
