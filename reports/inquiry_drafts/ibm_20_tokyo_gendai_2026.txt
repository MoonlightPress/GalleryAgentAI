Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji (Watercolor / Independent Artist)

Dear Tokyo Gendai Team,

I am writing to ask about the application process for exhibitors at Tokyo Gendai 2026, as I understand gallery and artist applications are currently open for the September edition. I am GEGYjiji, a Tokyo-based Chinese watercolor artist whose work centers on urban quiet, domestic interiors, and the particular light of Tokyo neighborhoods — themes I feel resonate with the considered, gallery-level programming Tokyo Gendai has built across its previous editions. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, grew from a daily watercolor diary practice I have maintained since 2020, and my work has been shown in Tokyo most recently as part of the group exhibition Tide from China at ACG_Labo in Harajuku. A selection of my work is visible on Instagram at @gegyjiji. I would be grateful to receive details on the application format, any applicable fees, and the submission deadline.

GEGYjiji
