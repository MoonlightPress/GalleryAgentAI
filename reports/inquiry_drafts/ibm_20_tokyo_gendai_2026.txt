Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Works on Paper

Dear Tokyo Gendai Team,

Having followed the fair's commitment to foregrounding process-driven and paper-based practices within Tokyo's contemporary art ecosystem, I am writing to inquire about exhibitor or gallery representation opportunities for the September 2026 edition. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work moves through the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds — transforming everyday urban moments into what I hope feel like emotionally resonant scenes. This practice grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary (2021), and continues as an ongoing daily watercolor diary I have maintained since 2020. My most recent Japan exhibition was Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku (February 2023), and a fuller picture of the work is available on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/. I would be grateful to learn more about the application process and whether my practice would be a good fit for Tokyo Gendai 2026.

Warm regards,
GEGYjiji
