Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Works on Paper

Dear Tokyo Gendai Team,

I am writing to inquire about the exhibitor application process for Tokyo Gendai 2026, which I understand is currently open for gallery and artist submissions. My name is GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work focuses on the ordinary scenes that accumulate into a life here — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street — transformed through daily watercolor painting into something that I hope feels both specific and quietly moving. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice called "diary," which became the foundation of my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary (October 2021), and my work was most recently shown in Japan as part of Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. A full view of the ongoing practice is at my Instagram, @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/). I would be grateful to know whether independent artists may apply directly, what the application requires, and whether the September 2026 dates remain confirmed.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
