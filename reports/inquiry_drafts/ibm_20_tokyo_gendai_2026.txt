Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Illustration

Dear Tokyo Gendai Team,

Tokyo Gendai has established itself as one of the few art fairs in Japan with a serious international program, and I am writing to ask about exhibitor or artist participation opportunities for the September 2026 edition. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work moves through the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular light of a familiar street corner — transforming everyday scenes into something emotionally resonant. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, grew from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020, and I made my Japan exhibition debut in February 2023 as part of Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku. A fuller picture of the work is on Instagram at @gegyjiji. Could you let me know the process and timeline for artist or exhibitor applications for 2026?

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
