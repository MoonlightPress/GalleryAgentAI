Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo

Dear Art Vancouver 2026 Team,

I came across your tenth edition through coverage highlighting the fair's commitment to showcasing diverse international voices alongside Canadian work, and I wanted to reach out to ask about the exhibitor application process for 2026. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my practice centers on finding something quietly extraordinary in the utterly ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, the particular quality of light in a familiar street — a sensibility that grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in 2021. I have maintained a daily watercolor practice called "diary" since 2020, and it is this sustained, observational approach to everyday life that defines the body of work I would bring to a booth context. You can see the current work on Instagram at @gegyjiji. I would be grateful to know whether applications for 2026 are still open, and if so, where I might find the submission details or fees.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
