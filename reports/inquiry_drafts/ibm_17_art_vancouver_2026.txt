Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo

Dear Art Vancouver 2026 Team,

I am writing to inquire about exhibitor opportunities for the 2026 edition, which I understand showcases contemporary art and design from both Canadian and international artists. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work moves through the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular light of a familiar street — transforming everyday scenes into emotionally resonant paintings. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, grew from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020, and this ongoing series reflects the same attentive, seasonal quality I would hope to bring to a booth context. I would be grateful to learn whether international exhibitor applications are still open, and what the submission process and associated fees look like. My work can be viewed on Instagram at @gegyjiji.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

GEGYjiji
