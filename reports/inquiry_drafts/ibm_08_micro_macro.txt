Subject: Exhibition Proposal — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Works

Dear MICRO/MACRO team,

Your program's interest in the tension between micro and macro perspectives — the close and the expansive held in the same frame — aligns closely with the concerns at the center of my watercolor practice. Since 2020, I have maintained a daily painting series called "diary," working from ordinary scenes: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, domestic interiors, the particular quality of light on a familiar street corner — the utterly everyday, rendered as something moving. This ongoing practice was the direct foundation for my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and it has continued to develop through my recent exhibition work, including Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku, Tokyo in February 2023. I would welcome the opportunity to present a body of these watercolor works at MICRO/MACRO, where I believe the intimacy of the images — their attention to small, specific things — would find a natural context within your exhibition format. A representative selection of my work is visible on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, and I am happy to provide a full portfolio, artist statement, or any additional materials at your request. I look forward to the possibility of being in conversation with your program.

GEGYjiji
