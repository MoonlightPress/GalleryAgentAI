Subject: Exhibition Proposal — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Works

Dear MICRO/MACRO team,

Your open call's framing of micro and macro perspectives aligns closely with what I find myself doing in watercolor: moving between the small and particular — a red wall catching afternoon light, a pool's surface, the way a familiar alleyway holds a whole season — and something larger that accumulates from those observations over time. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting practice called "diary," which grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work takes the utterly ordinary as its subject — street corners, domestic interiors, parks, green ponds — and tries to make those scenes feel like a place the viewer half-remembers, the way a train moving through the north carries all four seasons at once. In February 2023 I participated in Tide from China Part1, a group exhibition at ACG_Labo in Harajuku, Tokyo, which was my first exhibition in Japan. You can find a representative body of my daily work on Instagram at @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/), where I post consistently and where the cumulative quality of the practice is most visible. I would be glad to share a more focused portfolio or discuss how a selection of works might speak to the themes of your upcoming exhibition.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

GEGYjiji
