Subject: Exhibition Proposal — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Works

Dear MICRO/MACRO team,

Your program's focus on micro and macro perspectives — the way a single detail can open into something much larger — aligns closely with how I approach my own work, and I am writing to propose my watercolor paintings for consideration in an upcoming exhibition. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting practice in which I find moving scenes in the utterly ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner. This practice grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and continues as an ongoing series I call "diary." My work aims to give the viewer the sensation of walking through a place they half-remember — quiet, seasonal, grounded in specific images rather than abstraction. A selection of recent paintings can be viewed on my Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where I share new work daily. I would be glad to provide additional materials, a portfolio PDF, or any information your submission process requires.

Sincerely,
GEGYjiji
