Dear Blooming Stories team,

I came across your flower-themed open call running through June 29 and wanted to reach out, as my work feels closely aligned with the kind of attentive, observation-based practice your format seems to invite. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor series called "diary" — painting the ordinary scenes around me: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, grew directly from this practice. You can find a full view of the ongoing work on my Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/.
