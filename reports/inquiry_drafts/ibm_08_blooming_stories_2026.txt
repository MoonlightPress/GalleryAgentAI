Subject: Inquiry Regarding Blooming Stories 2026 — Watercolor Work by GEGYjiji

Dear Blooming Stories 2026 Team,

I came across your flower-themed open call and wanted to reach out, as the observation-based, quietly atmospheric work you seem to be looking for aligns closely with what I do. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice — a series called "diary" — built around the ordinary scenes I encounter: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular light of a familiar street corner. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary (October 2021), grew directly from that daily practice, and flowers and seasonal detail appear throughout it as part of the same attentive way of looking. I would be glad to know more about the submission process, any associated fees, and how work should be submitted before the June 29 deadline. You can find my current work on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/ — I update it daily.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.
