Subject: Inquiry Regarding Exhibition or Residency Opportunity — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Artist

Dear Koganecho Area Management Center,

I am writing because your work revitalizing the Koganecho neighborhood through artist programs — bringing sustained creative practice into the fabric of an evolving urban district — feels directly connected to what I do as a painter. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice called "diary," finding moving scenes in the utterly ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner — the kind of layered, lived-in spaces that Koganecho itself holds. My first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, grew directly from this daily practice, and my work was most recently shown in Japan as part of Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. I would be grateful to learn whether there are current or upcoming opportunities — exhibition, residency, or site-responsive project — through which this practice might find a place in your community. A fuller sense of my work is visible on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, and I am happy to share additional materials at your convenience.

Sincerely,
GEGYjiji
