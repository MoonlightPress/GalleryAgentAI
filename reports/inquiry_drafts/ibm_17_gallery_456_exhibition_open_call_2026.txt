Subject: Exhibition Proposal for Gallery 456 Open Call 2026 , GEGYjiji, Watercolor Illustrator

Dear Gallery 456 Team,

Gallery 456's open call for two-week exhibitions in 2026 feels like a genuine fit for my work, and I would like to propose a submission. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor illustrator based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province. My paintings tend to begin in quiet, familiar places, the kind of light that settles in a room in the afternoon, the feeling of a street you have walked down without quite noticing it. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice, which grew into my first solo collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and I made my first Japan exhibition in February 2023 as part of Tide from China Part 1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku, Tokyo. My ongoing daily work can be found at instagram.com/gegyjiji. I would be glad to share a full portfolio and discuss the space fees and scheduling details at your convenience.
