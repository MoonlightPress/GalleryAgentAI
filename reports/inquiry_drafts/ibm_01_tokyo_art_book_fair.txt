Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor / Colour Diary

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair team,

TABF's commitment to independent publishing and its reputation for bringing together artists whose work lives at the edge of illustration, zine culture, and fine art makes it a natural home for the kind of work I make. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice — a series called "diary" — that grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My paintings move through the ordinary: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the particular warmth of sunlight in a familiar street — transforming everyday scenes into something quietly moving. You can see the ongoing work at instagram.com/gegyjiji. I would be grateful to learn about the exhibitor application process, booth requirements, and fees for the next edition.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
