Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Artist

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair team,

Tokyo Art Book Fair has built a reputation as one of the few fairs in Japan where independently produced books and zines are treated with the same seriousness as gallery work, and it is that environment I am hoping to be part of. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice — a series called "diary" — that grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work moves through ordinary scenes: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner — things that are unremarkable until they become a painting. I would love to understand the current application process, booth format, and any relevant fees or deadlines for the next edition. My work can be seen on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/ and I am happy to provide any additional materials you require.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
