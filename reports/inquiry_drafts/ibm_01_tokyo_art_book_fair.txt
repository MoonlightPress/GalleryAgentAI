Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Artist

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair team,

TABF has established itself as one of the most serious platforms in Asia for artists whose work lives at the intersection of image-making and the printed page, and it is in that spirit that I am writing to ask about the exhibitor application process for the next edition. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice — a series called "diary" — that documents the utterly ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner. That practice led directly to my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, which I believe would sit naturally within the kind of intimate, image-led publishing that TABF tends to gather. You can find a continuous record of the work on my Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/. I would be grateful if you could share details on the application timeline, booth formats available, and any associated fees.

GEGYjiji
