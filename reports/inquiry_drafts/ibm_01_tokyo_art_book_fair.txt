Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair team,

TABF's reputation for bringing together independently published art books and zines with a genuine curatorial sensibility is what draws me to reach out about exhibiting at a future edition. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice — a series called "diary" — that grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work moves through the ordinary: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the warmth of sunlight in a familiar street — scenes that are entirely everyday and, I hope, quietly moving. You can find my work on Instagram at @gegyjiji. I would be grateful to learn more about the exhibitor application process, including deadlines, booth formats, and any fees, for the next edition.

GEGYjiji
