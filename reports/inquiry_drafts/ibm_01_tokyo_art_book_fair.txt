Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Artist

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair Team,

TABF's commitment to independent publishing and its platform for artists working at the intersection of image and print makes it exactly the kind of context I have been working toward. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice — a series called "diary" — that grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work moves through the ordinary: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the particular warmth of sunlight in a familiar street — transforming everyday scenes into something emotionally resonant. I would be grateful to learn about the exhibitor application process for the next fair, including deadlines, booth formats, and fees. You can find my ongoing work at Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/).

Thank you very much for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

GEGYjiji
