Subject: Exhibitor Application Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Artist

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair team,

Tokyo Art Book Fair's focus on independently produced artist books and the breadth of visual practices it brings together at MOT make it one of the few fairs where a watercolor diary-based publication feels genuinely at home. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting practice — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the ordinary scenes of a day transformed into something that lingers — which became the foundation of my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. I would like to inquire about the exhibitor application process for Tokyo Art Book Fair 2026, including the submission deadline, booth fees, and any eligibility requirements. My ongoing daily watercolor series, which I continue under the name "diary," also lends itself to zine and serial-format work that I would be glad to present at the fair. You can find my work on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/ and I am happy to provide further materials upon request.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

GEGYjiji
