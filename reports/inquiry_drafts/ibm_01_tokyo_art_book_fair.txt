Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Works & Colour Diary

Dear Tokyo Art Book Fair team,

TABF's reputation for bringing together independent publishers and artists whose work lives between image and object — and its home at the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo — makes it a context I have wanted to approach for some time. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting practice called "diary," which became the foundation for my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work moves through the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street — and tries to make those unremarkable moments feel like something the viewer half-remembers. You can find a sense of the work on Instagram at @gegyjiji. I would be grateful to learn whether exhibitor applications for the next edition are open, and what the submission process involves.

Thank you for your time.

GEGYjiji
