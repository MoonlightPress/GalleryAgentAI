Subject: Exhibition Proposal — GEGYjiji / Watercolor Works

Dear MICRO/MACRO team,

Your open call's focus on micro and macro perspectives caught my attention precisely because that tension — between the intimate and the expansive, the overlooked detail and the larger atmosphere it carries — sits at the center of my own practice. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting series called "diary," working from the ordinary scenes around me: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street. This practice led directly to my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and to my first Japan exhibition as part of Tide from China Part1 at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. My aim in each piece is to take what is utterly unremarkable — a corner, a puddle, the interior of a room — and render it in watercolor until it becomes a scene the viewer feels they half-remember. A selection of recent work is visible on my Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where the daily diary practice is also documented. I would be glad to discuss how my work might fit the exhibition and can provide a full portfolio, artist statement, or any additional materials you require.

GEGYjiji
