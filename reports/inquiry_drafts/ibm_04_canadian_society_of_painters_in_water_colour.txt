Subject: Submission Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Artist Based in Tokyo

Dear Canadian Society of Painters in Water Colour,

I am writing to inquire about submitting work to your open call ahead of the June 30, 2026 deadline. My name is GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my practice centers on the utterly ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds — transformed through watercolor into something emotionally resonant and seasonally alive. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice called "diary," which became the foundation for my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. You can see the ongoing work at my Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/), where I document this daily practice. I would be glad to provide any additional materials you require and look forward to the possibility of submitting to CSPWC.

GEGYjiji
