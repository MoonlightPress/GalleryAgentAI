Subject: Membership and Open Call Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Artist (Tokyo)

Dear Canadian Society of Painters in Water Colour,

I am writing because CSPWC's long-standing commitment to watercolor as a serious medium — and your tradition of welcoming international voices through open exhibitions — feels like a rare and genuinely fitting context for my work. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting practice called "diary," from which my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, grew directly in 2021. My paintings move through the ordinary: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the particular warmth of sunlight on a familiar street — scenes that carry all four seasons and give the viewer the feeling of walking through a place they half-remember. You can see this ongoing work at my Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/), where I post daily. I would be grateful to learn more about how international artists may participate in CSPWC's open calls or membership, and whether submissions for the 2026 cycle remain open.

GEGYjiji
