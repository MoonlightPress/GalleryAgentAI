Subject: Artist Residency Application — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo

Dear AIR-J Team,

Your database brings together some of the most thoughtful residency programs in Japan, and I am writing to submit my application through the open call listed at air-j.info. My name is GEGYjiji — a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province — and my practice centers on a daily series called "diary," which I have maintained without interruption since 2020: paintings of red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the particular quality of light in ordinary street corners, transforming the utterly familiar into something quietly moving. This daily practice grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and was also the foundation for my participation in Tide from China Part1, a group exhibition at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. A full record of my work is visible on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where the diary series is updated regularly. I would be glad to share a portfolio, artist statement, or any additional materials the selection committee requires.

GEGYjiji
