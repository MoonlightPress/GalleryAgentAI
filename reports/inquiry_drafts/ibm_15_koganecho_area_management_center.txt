Subject: Space Use Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Artist (Tokyo)

Dear Koganecho Area Management Center,

Koganecho's long commitment to site-responsive practice and the revitalization of everyday urban fabric through art is what brings me to write to you. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor practice — a series called "diary" — that moves through the ordinary: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the particular warmth of sunlight falling on a familiar street corner. This practice grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and I believe both the seriality of the work and its attentiveness to vernacular urban spaces speak directly to the kind of engagement Koganecho cultivates in the Hinodecho and Koganecho neighborhood. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss a possible residency or exhibition arrangement, and you are warmly invited to view my ongoing work at Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/). I would be grateful for any guidance on your current programs and how best to apply.

Sincerely,
GEGYjiji
