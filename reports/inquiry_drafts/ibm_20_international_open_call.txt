Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji, Watercolor Illustrator (Tokyo)

Dear Emma,

I came across the 2026 Kyoto Museum International Art Grand Exhibition open call and would like to submit my work for consideration. I am a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province, and my practice centers on the ordinary moments that accumulate into something quietly moving — red walls and alleyways, green ponds, the particular light of a familiar street corner. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor series called "diary," and this ongoing practice grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. In February 2023 I showed work as part of Tide from China Part1, a group exhibition at ACG_Labo in Harajuku, Tokyo, which marked my first exhibition in Japan. You can find a full picture of my practice and recent work on Instagram at @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/).

I would be glad to provide any additional materials or information you need.

GEGYjiji
