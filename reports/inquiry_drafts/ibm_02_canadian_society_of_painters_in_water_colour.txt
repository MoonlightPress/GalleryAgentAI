Subject: Entry Inquiry for the 2026 International Juried Exhibition

Dear Canadian Society of Painters in Water Colour,

I came across your open call for the 2026 international juried exhibition in transparent watercolour and wanted to reach out before the June 30 deadline. My name is GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor illustrator based in Tokyo. My work begins with a specific kind of ordinariness, the street corner I pass every morning, the way light sits on a familiar wall, the feeling of a place you know without quite thinking about it. Since 2020 I have kept a daily watercolor practice, which grew into my first solo collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. You can see my ongoing work at instagram.com/gegyjiji, and I would be glad to share more through a portfolio link below.
