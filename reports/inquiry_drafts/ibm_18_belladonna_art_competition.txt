Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji (Watercolor / Illustration)

Dear Belladonna Art Competition team,

I am writing to submit work for your current open call; the competition's focus on atmospheric, personal painting felt directly relevant to my practice. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor series called "diary" — paintings that move through the ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools and green ponds, the particular warmth of sunlight in a familiar street, transforming the utterly everyday into something quietly moving. This practice grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. A broader selection of my work is visible on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where I post from this ongoing daily practice. I would be glad to share any additional materials or higher-resolution files at your request.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

GEGYjiji
