Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Illustration

Dear Jinny Street Gallery,

I came across your open call and was drawn to the intimate, atmosphere-focused work you tend to show — it feels like a natural context for what I make. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting practice — a series called "diary" — that moves through ordinary scenes: red walls, alleyways, pools, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner, transforming the unremarkable everyday into something that stays with the viewer. That practice grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. A full record of my work is on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where I currently share with around 90,000 followers. I would be glad to send selected works, files, or any other materials you need for consideration.

GEGYjiji
