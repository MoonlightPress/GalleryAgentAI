Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji (Watercolor / Illustration)

Dear ARRIVAL team,

Your Osaka open call caught my attention because the work you platform tends toward the still and atmospheric — the kind of image that holds a single ordinary moment long enough for something in it to shift. My own practice moves through exactly that territory: red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, the light in a familiar street corner, the interior of a room — watercolor scenes drawn from daily life that I have been painting without interruption since 2020 under the title "diary." That ongoing daily practice grew directly into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and was also the foundation of my participation in Tide from China Part1, a group exhibition at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. You can see a continuous record of the work at Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/). I would be glad to share a portfolio or any additional materials your submission process requires, and welcome any details about fees, eligibility, or submission format at your convenience.

GEGYjiji
