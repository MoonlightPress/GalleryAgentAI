Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo

Dear ARRIVAL Team,

I came across your Osaka open call and wanted to submit my work for consideration — your focus on intimate, atmospheric art feels closely aligned with what I make. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and since 2020 I have maintained a daily painting practice called "diary," finding moving scenes in the utterly ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, the particular warmth of light on a familiar street corner. That practice became my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and continues to shape everything I make. A representative selection of my work is on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where I post daily. I would be glad to share any additional materials — portfolio PDFs, print dimensions, or exhibition history — and to learn more about your submission requirements and any associated fees.

Thank you for your time and for running this opportunity.

GEGYjiji
