Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo

Dear Art Vancouver 2026 Team,

I am writing to ask about the exhibitor application process for Art Vancouver 2026, which I understand showcases contemporary art and design from both Canadian and international artists. My name is GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo; my work moves through the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular light of a familiar street corner — and transforms these everyday scenes into emotionally resonant paintings. In October 2021 I published my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, which grew directly from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020. You can find a broad view of that ongoing work on my Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where I paint and post daily. I would be grateful to know the submission details, any associated fees, and whether international exhibitors are welcome to apply.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to hearing from you.

GEGYjiji
