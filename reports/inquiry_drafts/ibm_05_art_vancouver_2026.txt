Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo

Dear Art Vancouver team,

Art Vancouver's history of bringing together international contemporary artists alongside Canadian voices makes it a fair I've followed with genuine interest, and the tenth edition feels like a meaningful moment to reach out. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work finds its subject matter in the ordinary — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a room or a street corner — and tries to make those scenes feel moving rather than merely observed. In October 2021 I published my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, which grew directly from a daily watercolor practice I have maintained since 2020, and that ongoing series continues to be the core of what I make. I would be grateful to learn whether exhibitor applications for 2026 are still open, and what the submission process involves. A sense of my work is at instagram.com/gegyjiji.

GEGYjiji
