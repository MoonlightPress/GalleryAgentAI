Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Tokyo Gendai 2026

Dear Tokyo Gendai team,

Tokyo Gendai has established itself as one of the few art fairs in Japan that takes seriously the conversation between international contemporary practice and the Tokyo audience, and I am writing to ask about the process for independent artists or smaller presentations to participate in the 2026 edition. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work turns the unremarkable fabric of daily life — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the light in a familiar room — into paintings that I hope give the viewer the feeling of walking through a place they half-remember. This practice has been running continuously since 2020 as a daily series called "diary," and it formed the foundation of my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in 2021, as well as my first Japan group exhibition, Tide from China Part1, at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. My current work and ongoing diary can be viewed on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/. I would be grateful to understand what application or inquiry route is appropriate for an artist at my stage, and whether there are formats within Tokyo Gendai 2026 suited to a body of work in this scale and register.

Warm regards,
GEGYjiji
