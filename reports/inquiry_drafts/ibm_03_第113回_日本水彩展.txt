Subject: Inquiry Regarding Submission to the 113th Japan Watercolor Exhibition

Dear 日本水彩展 Organizing Committee,

I am writing to inquire about the submission process and deadline for the 113th Japan Watercolor Exhibition, which I understand is a major open-call exhibition dedicated to watercolor in Japan. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, originally from Hunan Province, and watercolor has been my primary medium since I began a daily painting practice in 2020 — a series called "diary" that gave rise to my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021. My work turns toward the ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street — scenes that might be overlooked, made into something that stays with the viewer. In February 2023 I participated in Tide from China Part 1, a group exhibition at ACG_Labo in Harajuku, which was my first exhibition in Japan. My ongoing work can be seen on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/ — I would be grateful for any information on how to submit and the relevant deadlines for the next edition.

Sincerely,
GEGYjiji
