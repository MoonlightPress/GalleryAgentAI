Subject: Inquiry Regarding Submission to the 113th Japan Watercolor Exhibition

Dear 日本水彩展 Organizing Committee,

I am writing to inquire about the submission process and deadline for the 114th Japan Watercolor Exhibition, as I was unable to find these details listed publicly on the website. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my work grows from a daily painting practice — a series called "diary" — that documents the ordinary: red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, and the quiet light of familiar streets transformed into something moving. This practice led directly to my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and to my participation in Tide from China Part 1, a group exhibition held at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. The Japan Watercolor Exhibition's long commitment to the medium makes it a natural context for this work, and I would be glad to know what is required to submit. My ongoing watercolor diary can be viewed at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/.

Thank you for your time, and I look forward to your response.

GEGYjiji
