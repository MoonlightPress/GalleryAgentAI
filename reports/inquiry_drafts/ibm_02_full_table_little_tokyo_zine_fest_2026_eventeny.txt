Subject: Exhibitor Inquiry — Little Tokyo Zine Fest 2026

Dear Little Tokyo Zine Fest team,

Little Tokyo Zine Fest's focus on small-edition print work and intimate artist tables feels like the right context for what I make: daily watercolor paintings that move through ordinary scenes — red walls, alleyways, pools, the particular light of a familiar street corner — and try to make something emotionally true from them. Since 2020 I have kept a daily watercolor practice called "diary," and my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary (October 2021), grew directly from that habit of looking slowly at everyday life. I would like to apply for a Full-Table vendor spot at the 2026 event and would be grateful to know the application process, table fee, and whether a finished publication is required to apply. Recent work is on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/ if it helps to see the practice before we correspond further. Please let me know what the next step looks like — I am happy to send any additional materials you need.
