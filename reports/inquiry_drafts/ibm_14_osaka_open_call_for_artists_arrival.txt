Subject: Open Call Submission — GEGYjiji, Watercolor / Illustration

Dear ARRIVAL Team,

Your Osaka open call draws artists working across disciplines and geographies, and I am writing to submit my work for consideration. I am GEGYjiji, a Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo, and my practice centers on the ordinary scenes most people walk past without stopping — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar room — which I have been painting daily since 2020 in an ongoing series called "diary." That sustained daily observation became the foundation of my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and led to my first Japan group exhibition, Tide from China Part1, at ACG_Labo in Harajuku in February 2023. A full view of my work is on Instagram at https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/, where I share the diary paintings regularly. I would be glad to provide portfolio files, artist statement, or any additional materials your selection process requires.

GEGYjiji
