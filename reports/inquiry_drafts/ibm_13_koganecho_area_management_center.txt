Subject: Inquiry Regarding Exhibition or Residency Opportunity — GEGYjiji (Watercolor Artist, Tokyo)

Dear Koganecho Area Management Center,

I write with admiration for the work you have sustained in Kogane-cho — the way your programming treats the neighborhood itself as living subject, drawing artists into genuine conversation with its streets and residents, is an approach I find deeply aligned with my own practice. Since 2020 I have maintained a daily watercolor series called "diary," painting the ordinary scenes that surround me — red walls, alleyways, pools, green ponds, the particular quality of light in a familiar street corner — with the aim of making the utterly unremarkable feel moving and worth dwelling in. This practice grew into my first solo illustration collection, Colour Diary, published in October 2021, and I believe the same attentiveness that drives that work would respond well to Kogane-cho's layered urban landscape. I am writing to inquire whether you have current or forthcoming opportunities — exhibition, residency, or site-responsive project — for which my work might be considered, and I would be glad to share further materials at your request. My watercolor practice can be followed at Instagram @gegyjiji (https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/).

With respect and gratitude for your time,
GEGYjiji
