# Portfolio Pitch — 新進芸術家海外研修制度（特別）

## Recommended Body of Work
Architecture / Memory / Place

## Why This Body Fits
Quiet images of buildings, streets, interiors, fragments, and places that feel emotionally charged or half-remembered.

## Opportunity Fit
This government fellowship supports emerging visual artists conducting overseas research—watercolor illustration qualifies—and offers up to ¥1.5M, making it substantial enough to fund an extended study residency abroad if your daily practice has developed a specific research direction you want to deepen outside Japan. Check the submission deadline and requirements on their official page, then map out whether a focused overseas research proposal (tied to your urban/architectural work) would strengthen your application before you submit.

## Match Signals
urban

## Suggested Submission Framing
Present this not as a general portfolio, but as a focused body of work.

The pitch should emphasize:
- quiet atmosphere
- memory and place
- visual consistency
- publication or sequence potential
- emotional restraint
- sensitivity to everyday spaces

## Recommended Materials
- 8–12 image sequence
- short artist statement
- short project statement
- website / Instagram / PDF portfolio
- 1 paragraph explaining why this venue or publication fits the work

## Caution
Do not send too much. The first contact should feel precise, not desperate or overfull.
