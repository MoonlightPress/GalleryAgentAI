# Portfolio Pitch — 新進芸術家海外研修制度

## Recommended Body of Work
Architecture / Memory / Place

## Why This Body Fits
Quiet images of buildings, streets, interiors, fragments, and places that feel emotionally charged or half-remembered.

## Opportunity Fit
新進芸術家海外研修制度 is a government grant explicitly for emerging visual artists seeking overseas research funding—no gallery representation required—and your daily watercolor practice and documented focus on urban architecture make you eligible to apply; check the submission deadline and requirements now, then prepare a project proposal framing how overseas research would deepen your work on memory and disappearing moments in built environments.

## Match Signals
architecture, memory, urban

## Suggested Submission Framing
Present this not as a general portfolio, but as a focused body of work.

The pitch should emphasize:
- quiet atmosphere
- memory and place
- visual consistency
- publication or sequence potential
- emotional restraint
- sensitivity to everyday spaces

## Recommended Materials
- 8–12 image sequence
- short artist statement
- short project statement
- website / Instagram / PDF portfolio
- 1 paragraph explaining why this venue or publication fits the work

## Caution
Do not send too much. The first contact should feel precise, not desperate or overfull.
