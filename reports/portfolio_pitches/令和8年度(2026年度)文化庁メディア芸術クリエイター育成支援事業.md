# Portfolio Pitch — 令和8年度（2026年度）文化庁メディア芸術クリエイター育成支援事業

## Recommended Body of Work
Daily Poetic Photography

## Why This Body Fits
A quieter observational body of work built around daily life, light, domestic details, travel fragments, and subtle emotional atmosphere.

## Opportunity Fit
This is a Japanese government grant for visual arts creators—no application fee, based in Tokyo where you're already working, and designed to support ongoing practice development rather than finished exhibitions. Check the eligibility requirements (residency, citizenship status) on the Bunka-chō site, and if you qualify, prepare 8–12 pages of your daily watercolor diary work and a brief statement on your urban observation practice to submit before the deadline.

## Match Signals
daily, observation

## Suggested Submission Framing
Present this not as a general portfolio, but as a focused body of work.

The pitch should emphasize:
- quiet atmosphere
- memory and place
- visual consistency
- publication or sequence potential
- emotional restraint
- sensitivity to everyday spaces

## Recommended Materials
- 8–12 image sequence
- short artist statement
- short project statement
- website / Instagram / PDF portfolio
- 1 paragraph explaining why this venue or publication fits the work

## Caution
Do not send too much. The first contact should feel precise, not desperate or overfull.
