# Portfolio Pitch — 第九屆水主題國際評審藝術比賽

## Recommended Body of Work
Architecture / Memory / Place

## Why This Body Fits
Quiet images of buildings, streets, interiors, fragments, and places that feel emotionally charged or half-remembered.

## Opportunity Fit
第九屆水主題國際評審藝術比賽's focus on water as subject matter could be a platform for architectural watercolors, where water often appears as reflective surface and atmospheric element in your urban Tokyo scenes. The competition's emphasis on the medium itself—watercolor as both technique and conceptual vehicle—directly validates your daily practice of capturing ephemeral light and memory through translucent washes on paper.

## Match Signals
memory, urban, atmospheric

## Suggested Submission Framing
Present this not as a general portfolio, but as a focused body of work.

The pitch should emphasize:
- quiet atmosphere
- memory and place
- visual consistency
- publication or sequence potential
- emotional restraint
- sensitivity to everyday spaces

## Recommended Materials
- 8–12 image sequence
- short artist statement
- short project statement
- website / Instagram / PDF portfolio
- 1 paragraph explaining why this venue or publication fits the work

## Caution
Do not send too much. The first contact should feel precise, not desperate or overfull.
