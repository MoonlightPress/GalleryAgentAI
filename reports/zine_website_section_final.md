# Final Website Section: Zines / Artist Books

This is the lowest-friction career path: make a small printed object, place it in a few real shops, and use those placements as proof-of-activity for larger fairs, galleries, and publishers.

## Top Stats

- Timeline: 1-3 months

## Why This Path
The zine path is currently the fastest route to public visibility. There are 12 identified targets, including 7 in Koenji/Nakano. Costs are relatively low, and one printed project can be reused for shop placement, applications, sales, fairs, portfolio proof, and later publishing outreach.

## Practical Solution
- Select one coherent body of work.
- Create two small zines: one image-led, one quieter narrative/atmosphere sequence.
- Print 20 copies of each.
- Visit or contact the five Phase 1 targets.
- Track whether each accepts consignment, direct purchase, event participation, or no submissions.
- Use any placement as proof-of-activity for fairs, publishers, and galleries.
