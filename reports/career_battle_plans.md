# Career Battle Plans

Channel-level action plans. This is meant to answer: what should an artist do with this ecosystem?

## Zines
- Coverage: usable initial map
- Maximum impact: Place one finished zine in 3-5 local shops and use those placements as proof-of-activity.
- 60-day goal: Print 20 copies and secure 1-3 shop placements.
- Map target: 100 Tokyo entries; 3 Koenji; 1 Nakano; 10 within 20km of Koenji.

### Build the object
- Select one coherent theme.
- Choose 12-20 images or pages.
- Make one finished zine PDF.
- Print 20 test copies.

### Local placement
- Map 3 Koenji shops, 1 Nakano shop, and 10 nearby shops.
- Visit the nearest 3 with one sample copy.
- Ask whether they accept consignment, small press zines, or artist books.
- Track response status.

### Expansion
- Approach 5 additional shops.
- Prepare a short bilingual description.
- Post only after a placement or real action occurs.

### Metrics
- shops researched
- shops visited
- copies placed
- copies sold
- follow-up dates

## Artist Books
- Coverage: usable initial map
- Maximum impact: Turn the strongest body of work into one artist-book prototype, then use it for TABF-style applications.
- 60-day goal: Create one prototype and compare it against 5 similar artist books.
- Map target: 40 Tokyo entries; 1 Koenji; 1 Nakano; 8 within 20km of Koenji.

### Prototype
- Choose one body of work.
- Create a 10-20 page sequence.
- Export a print-ready PDF.
- Photograph the mockup.

### Benchmark
- Find 5 comparable artist books.
- Note size, price, page count, binding, and presentation.
- Identify what makes her object distinct.

### Applications
- Prepare TABF application materials.
- Shortlist 3 artist-book fairs or bookstores.
- Prepare one concise project statement.

### Metrics
- prototype completed
- comparable books reviewed
- applications prepared
- stores/fairs researched

## Illustration Galleries
- Coverage: usable initial map
- Maximum impact: Use Pinpoint-style opportunities as the formal exhibition bridge.
- 60-day goal: Prepare one strong PDF and submit to the best-fit illustration/gallery opportunity.
- Map target: 30 Tokyo entries; 1 Koenji; 1 Nakano; 10 within 20km of Koenji.

### Portfolio
- Select 8-12 strongest works.
- Order them into a coherent visual sequence.
- Write a 100-word artist statement.
- Create one PDF portfolio.

### Fit check
- Review 10 artists from Pinpoint or similar galleries.
- Compare medium, tone, subject, and presentation.
- Decide whether the opportunity is truly similar or only adjacent.

### Submission
- Confirm deadline and fee.
- Prepare application images.
- Submit or mark as not worth it.

### Metrics
- portfolio completed
- artists reviewed
- applications submitted
- responses received

## Small Press Publishers
- Coverage: usable initial map
- Maximum impact: Build a shortlist of publishers who actually handle artist books, zines, or illustration-adjacent editions.
- 60-day goal: Research 20, shortlist 5, contact 2.
- Map target: 50 Tokyo entries; 1 Koenji; 1 Nakano; 12 within 20km of Koenji.

### Research
- Collect 20 publishers/presses.
- Tag each by country, style, openness, and contact method.
- Reject generic or poor-fit publishers.

### Shortlist
- Choose 5 that match her work.
- Find comparable artists/books they published.
- Prepare one specific reason for each.

### Contact
- Send 2 careful emails.
- Attach portfolio or artist-book PDF only when appropriate.
- Track responses.

### Metrics
- publishers researched
- publishers shortlisted
- emails sent
- responses received

## Print Markets
- Coverage: partial map
- Maximum impact: Test which images people will buy before investing too much in large editions.
- 60-day goal: Prepare 10 prints and test one small market or popup.
- Map target: 25 Tokyo entries; 1 Koenji; 1 Nakano; 8 within 20km of Koenji.

### Product test
- Select 10 works.
- Make small print editions.
- Set prices.
- Prepare basic packaging.

### Market test
- Find 5 local print/zine/design markets.
- Choose one realistic event.
- Prepare table/display checklist.

### Metrics
- prints prepared
- markets researched
- events applied to
- sales

## Instagram / TikTok
- Coverage: needs expansion
- Maximum impact: Use social platforms to document real progress, not as a substitute for career actions.
- 60-day goal: Create a simple posting system tied to zines, shop visits, and finished work.

### Content base
- Prepare 10 image posts.
- Prepare 3 process videos.
- Prepare 3 shop/zine/story posts.

### Tie to real actions
- Post when a zine is printed.
- Post when a shop visit happens.
- Post when a piece is placed or exhibited.

### Metrics
- posts prepared
- process videos prepared
- real-world actions documented
- portfolio link clicks
