# Artist Packet Templates

## 8-12 Image Portfolio PDF Structure

1. Cover page: artist name, project title, contact/website
2. Short statement: 80-120 words
3. 8-12 works, one per page
4. Caption under each work: title, year, medium, dimensions
5. Final page: short bio and links

## Gallery Inquiry Email Structure

- 1 sentence introduction
- 1 sentence why this venue fits
- 1 sentence about the work
- Portfolio link
- Polite question: whether they accept portfolio review or exhibition proposals

## Open Exhibition Checklist

- Confirm current deadline
- Confirm eligible medium
- Confirm image size requirements
- Confirm fee
- Confirm location/shipping/framing rules
- Confirm rights/usage terms

## Book/Zine Fair Checklist

- Is there a real book/zine object?
- Are sample spreads ready?
- Is there a price/edition plan?
- Is the table fee worth it?
- Does this help the artist meet the right audience?
