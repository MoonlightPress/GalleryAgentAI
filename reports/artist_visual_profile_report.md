# Artist Visual Profile

GEGYjiji is a 26-year-old Chinese watercolor artist based in Tokyo. Originally from Changsha, she paints urban atmosphere, domestic stillness, cats, interior light, and the quiet accumulation of memory. Her work is intimate in scale and emotionally precise. She has N2 Japanese and 90k Twitter followers, and is actively seeking gallery exhibition opportunities and publishing collaborations. She needs a mix of prestigious international opportunities and intimate local Tokyo venues.
