# Watercolor Project Plan

This replaces the photography-first project logic with watercolor-first project logic.

## Quiet Neighborhood Watercolors

**Format:** 12–18 watercolor paintings

A coherent series of small watercolors based on ordinary neighborhoods, plants, shopfronts, windows, stairs, fences, and lived-in architectural details.

**Portfolio use:** Best for works-on-paper open calls, bookstore/gallery spaces, and small gallery submissions.

### Best Targets
- **TOKYO ART BOOK FAIR** — watercolor score 10. Tokyo location and alternative art community access align well, and your documentary approach could translate to artist book / watercolor sequence or artist book format if that medium interests you.
- **UTRECHT** — watercolor score 10. This sidesteps your strategic risk entirely—bookstore galleries celebrate painterly work, your the work could translate well in print format, and there's no pressure to perform conceptually or network aggressively.
- **B&B Shimokitazawa** — watercolor score 10. Aligns with your comfort for low-key cultural spaces and Japan focus, but unclear if they program watercolor artists without publication angle
- **flotsam books** — watercolor score 10. Tokyo location aligns with regional interests; bookshop/gallery hybrid matches comfort with lower-pressure exhibition contexts
- **Comitia** — watercolor score 10. It doesn't. This is a doujinshi/self-publishing event that would pull you away from the institutional and research-oriented positioning you're building.
- **Book and Sons** — watercolor score 10. Bookstore galleries attract viewers who value careful observation and cultural documentation - your work's literary quality and attention to disappearing spaces aligns with this audience's sensibility.
- **NADiff a/p/a/r/t** — watercolor score 10. Geographic fit with Tokyo location and art book culture, but unclear if they accept artist submissions at all
- **Photobook Cafe** — watercolor score 10. Potentially strong for a quiet artist book / watercolor sequence-oriented artist seeking community, visibility, and publication context.

### First Action
Choose 12 finished or nearly finished watercolors. Group them by place, plant life, architecture, and human trace.

## Human Trace / Works on Paper

**Format:** focused works-on-paper proposal

A tighter project about indirect human presence: domestic edges, plants, walls, windows, shop signs, objects, and architectural traces.

**Portfolio use:** Best for group shows, open exhibitions, and residencies where the work needs a clearer conceptual statement.

### Best Targets
- **flotsam books** — watercolor score 10. Tokyo location aligns with regional interests; bookshop/gallery hybrid matches comfort with lower-pressure exhibition contexts
- **Comitia** — watercolor score 10. It doesn't. This is a doujinshi/self-publishing event that would pull you away from the institutional and research-oriented positioning you're building.
- **Photobook Cafe** — watercolor score 10. Potentially strong for a quiet artist book / watercolor sequence-oriented artist seeking community, visibility, and publication context.
- **AIR 3331** — watercolor score 10. Tokyo location directly supports documentation of Asian urban spaces; established venue provides institutional credibility without elite-level conceptual demands; research-oriented context matches your preferred residency type
- **BankART1929** — watercolor score 10. Their focus on preservation of historical urban spaces through contemporary art mirrors your cultural documentation approach, and Yokohama's proximity to Tokyo positions this well regionally.
- **Youkobo Art Space** — watercolor score 10. Tokyo location aligns with your subject matter and Asia-Pacific focus, but unclear program structure creates uncertainty about actual compatibility.
- **Tokyo Wonder Site / TOKAS Residency** — watercolor score 10. TOKAS supports serious cultural research in Tokyo, which matches your documentation-based practice and subject matter. The institutional legitimacy would strengthen your positioning as a researcher rather than watercolor artist.
- **Aperture | Photography** — watercolor score 10. Potential fit based on source context and keyword overlap: artist book / watercolor sequence, zine, watercolor / works on paper, exhibition

### First Action
Write a 90-word statement and select 8–12 works that all show indirect human presence.
