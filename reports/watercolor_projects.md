# Watercolor Project Plan

This replaces the photography-first project logic with watercolor-first project logic.

## Quiet Neighborhood Watercolors

**Format:** 12–18 watercolor paintings

A coherent series of small watercolors based on ordinary neighborhoods, plants, shopfronts, windows, stairs, fences, and lived-in architectural details.

**Portfolio use:** Best for works-on-paper open calls, bookstore/gallery spaces, and small gallery submissions.
