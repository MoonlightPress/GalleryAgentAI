## CRITICAL VERIFICATION CHECKLIST

**Before ANY outreach:**
- [ ] Confirm original medium (watercolor/gouache vs. digital)
- [ ] Determine artist location/nationality (affects gallery fit)
- [ ] Check existing representation status (exclusivity conflicts?)
- [ ] Verify work dimensions (affects pricing/shipping)
- [ ] Confirm exhibition CV strength (affects gallery tier)
- [ ] Reverse image search artworks (already commercially placed?)
- [ ] Check artist Instagram follower count/engagement (indicates audience)

## 90-DAY ACTION SEQUENCE

**Month 1:**
1. Confirm medium → adjust pricing strategy
2. Launch Saatchi Art account (15 images)
3. Get 3 giclée printer quotes
4. Research KAYOKOYUKI + Thinkspace current programs

**Month 2:**
5. Produce first print edition (2 images, 50 each)
6. Email KAYOKOYUKI portfolio submission
7. Apply Artsy if Saatchi shows traction
8. Monitor Saatchi analytics (traffic, favorites, sales)

**Month 3:**
9. Submit Thinkspace if KAYOKOYUKI non-responsive
10. Apply TOLOT open call if available
11. Research AAF HK 2025 exhibitors
12. Evaluate: If online sales strong → continue; if weak → reassess pricing/positioning

**Success metrics:** 1 gallery response OR $2K+ online sales OR 500+ platform favorites = strategy validated
