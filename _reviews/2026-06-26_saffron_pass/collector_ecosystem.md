# Collector Ecosystem

*Who actually buys work like yours — and the calm, optional ways to let them.*

This is not a to-do list and nothing here is urgent. You are already doing the thing that matters most: making the work and sharing it daily. The notes below just gather, in one place, the real places where people who love watercolor originals and artist-books tend to gather — and a few low-pressure ways to make it easy for the people who already love your work to buy it, *if and when you ever feel like it.*

收藏者生态 — 谁真正会买你这样的作品，以及一些平静、随意、毫无压力的方式让他们买到。

这不是任务清单，也没有任何一件事是紧急的。你已经在做最重要的事：持续创作、每天分享。下面只是把"喜欢水彩原作和艺术书的人通常聚集在哪里"整理到一处，再附上几条低压力的小方法，方便那些**已经喜欢你作品的人**买到它——**只在你想做的时候做就好。**

---

## 1. Where collectors of work like yours actually are

These are real, verified platforms. For each: who's there, how it works, and how realistic it is for you specifically. Nothing here is ranked, and you don't need all of them — most artists thrive on one or two that feel natural.

下面都是真实、已核实的平台。每一条都说明：谁在这里、怎么运作、对你来说是否现实。这里没有排名，你也不需要全部用上——大多数艺术家只靠一两个用着顺手的渠道就活得很好。

### Instagram — direct (DM to inquire)
**Link:** https://www.instagram.com/gegyjiji/

- **Who's here / how it works:** This is, realistically, your single biggest collector channel already. The people who follow your daily watercolor diary are exactly the people who would buy an original, a zine, or a postcard set. The way it works is quiet: someone sees a piece they love, sends a DM asking "is this available?", and you reply. No storefront, no pressure — just a message.
- **Realistic for you?** Yes — more than any other channel on this page. Your audience is already here and already engaged. The only gentle, optional addition is making it *obvious* that inquiries are welcome (see the how-to below).

**Instagram — 私信咨询**
- **谁在这里 / 怎么运作：** 现实地说，这已经是你最大的收藏渠道。关注你每日水彩日记的人，正是会买原作、画册或明信片的人。运作方式很安静：有人看到喜欢的作品，发私信问"这幅还在吗？"，你回复即可。不需要店面，没有压力，只是一条消息。
- **对你现实吗？** 是的——比本页任何渠道都更现实。你的观众已经在这里，已经在互动。唯一可加的、完全随意的一步，是让大家**清楚知道**欢迎咨询（见下方"怎么做"）。

### BASE — your own shop (you already use this)
**Link:** https://gegyjiji.base.shop/ · Platform: https://thebase.com/

- **Who's here / how it works:** Your existing shop. BASE is a free Japanese platform for running your own storefront (you only pay a fee when something sells), with built-in Instagram linking. Your shop already does the most important things well: originals, zines, postcards, and clear **SOLD OUT** marking on the top pieces.
- **Realistic for you?** It's already real and already working — three originals at your top price (¥115,500) have sold. This is your calm "buy button" so that buying never depends only on catching you in DMs. Nothing to change; it's the quiet anchor everything else can point to.

**BASE — 你自己的店铺（你已经在用）**
- **谁在这里 / 怎么运作：** 你现有的店铺。BASE 是日本的免费开店平台（卖出才收费），并自带 Instagram 链接。你的店铺已经把最重要的事做得很好：原作、画册、明信片，以及高价作品清晰的 **SOLD OUT（售罄）** 标记。
- **对你现实吗？** 它已经真实存在并已在运作——三幅最高价（¥115,500）的原作已售出。这是你平静的"购买按钮"，让购买不必只依赖于在私信里恰好找到你。无需改动，它是其他一切都可以指向的安静锚点。

### Tokyo Art Book Fair (TABF)
**Link:** https://tokyoartbookfair.com/

- **Who's here / how it works:** The largest art-book and zine fair in Asia, held at the Museum of Contemporary Art Tokyo. The audience is exactly your native ecosystem — people who collect zines, artist books, and printed matter, and who buy on the spot. Artists apply to exhibit (table) for a cycle; it's a once-a-year event, not an everyday channel.
- **Realistic for you?** Very fitting in spirit — your zines (*Colour Diary*, *Komorebi*, *Light, Shadow, Cats*) are made for exactly this room, and you already exhibit in Tokyo. It's a bigger commitment than an online listing (an application and a table), so it's a "someday, if you want to" rather than a today thing.

**东京艺术书展（TABF）**
- **谁在这里 / 怎么运作：** 亚洲最大的艺术书与 zine 书展，在东京都现代美术馆举办。观众正是你的本土生态——收藏 zine、艺术书和印刷品、并会当场购买的人。艺术家申请参展（摆摊）；这是一年一度的活动，不是日常渠道。
- **对你现实吗？** 在精神上非常契合——你的画册（《色彩日记》《Komorebi》《和光和影和猫》）就是为这样的场合而做的，而你本就在东京参展。它比线上上架要投入更多（需要申请和摆摊），所以是"某天，如果你想"，而不是今天的事。

### Creema — Japanese handmade & art marketplace
**Link:** https://www.creema.jp/

- **Who's here / how it works:** One of Japan's largest creator marketplaces, skewing toward higher unit prices and "works for adults" — buyers in their 20s–40s looking for quality original work, including art and illustration. You list items; the platform brings the audience.
- **Realistic for you?** A natural fit for zines, postcards, and prints, and reasonable for smaller originals. It reaches Japanese buyers who may never have found your Instagram. Fully optional and low-effort — a listing can sit there quietly.

**Creema — 日本手作与艺术市集**
- **谁在这里 / 怎么运作：** 日本最大的创作者市集之一，客单价偏高、偏向"成年人的作品"——20–40 岁、寻找高质量原创（包括艺术与插画）的买家。你上架商品，平台带来观众。
- **对你现实吗？** 很适合画册、明信片和版画，小幅原作也合理。它能触达可能从未发现你 Instagram 的日本买家。完全随意、几乎不费力——上架后可以安静地放着。

### minne — Japan's largest handmade marketplace
**Link:** https://minne.com/

- **Who's here / how it works:** Japan's biggest handmade marketplace, very beginner-friendly to set up. Broad audience, lots of casual browsing; strong for accessible price points like postcards and zines.
- **Realistic for you?** A gentle, no-commitment option that pairs well with Creema if you ever want a second Japanese storefront for the affordable tier. Not essential — listed so you know it exists.

**minne — 日本最大的手作市集**
- **谁在这里 / 怎么运作：** 日本最大的手作市集，开店非常适合新手。受众广、随意浏览多；对明信片、画册这类亲民价位尤其有利。
- **对你现实吗？** 一个温和、零承诺的选项，若你想为亲民价位再开一个日本店面，它与 Creema 可以互补。并非必需——列在这里只是让你知道它存在。

### BOOTH (pixiv) — illustration-community marketplace
**Link:** https://booth.pm/en

- **Who's here / how it works:** pixiv's creator marketplace — the heart of the illustration community you came up in. Free to open a shop, English interface available, and it handles both physical goods (zines, prints, postcards) and digital. The audience is illustration-native and already used to buying directly from artists.
- **Realistic for you?** A good cultural fit given your illustration-community roots. Best for zines, prints, and postcards; most shipping is within Japan. Optional, but it speaks your first language as an artist.

**BOOTH（pixiv）— 插画社区市集**
- **谁在这里 / 怎么运作：** pixiv 的创作者市集——你成长所在的插画社区的核心。免费开店，有英文界面，既能卖实体（画册、版画、明信片）也能卖数字内容。受众本就是插画圈、习惯直接向艺术家购买。
- **对你现实吗？** 鉴于你的插画社区根基，文化上很契合。最适合画册、版画和明信片；多数运送仅限日本国内。可选，但它说的是你作为艺术家的"母语"。

### Bookshop-gallery consignment (e.g. UTRECHT)
**Link:** https://utrecht.jp/

- **Who's here / how it works:** Tokyo has a small network of art bookshops that stock self-published artist books on consignment — UTRECHT (in Shibuya, and a co-host of TABF) is the well-known anchor. You bring or send copies of a zine; they sell them and take a cut; the shop's existing art-loving foot traffic does the discovery for you.
- **Realistic for you?** A quiet, ambient way for your zines to live in the city without you performing or pitching. It's a short, friendly email to ask about their consignment policy — no exhibition, no deadline. A natural fit for your printed work.

**书店画廊寄售（例如 UTRECHT）**
- **谁在这里 / 怎么运作：** 东京有一小批艺术书店以寄售方式代销自出版艺术书——UTRECHT（位于涩谷，也是 TABF 的共同主办方）是知名的代表。你把 zine 送去或寄去，他们代售并抽成；书店本身爱艺术的客流会替你完成"被发现"这一步。
- **对你现实吗？** 一种安静、自然的方式，让你的画册留在这座城市里，而你无需表演或推销。只需一封简短友好的邮件询问他们的寄售政策——不必办展、没有截止日期。很适合你的印刷作品。

## 2. The lowest-pressure how-to

If you ever feel like making it easier for the people who already love your work to buy it, here is the calmest possible path. Each step is optional and reversible. You can stop after step one.

如果你哪天想让那些已经喜欢你作品的人更容易买到它，下面是最平静的一条路径。每一步都是可选、可撤销的。做完第一步就停下也完全可以。

1. **Keep one link in your bio that goes to your shop.** Just your https://gegyjiji.base.shop/ link in your Instagram bio, so anyone who loves a piece can find where to buy without asking. This is the single highest-value, lowest-effort step. / **在简介里放一个指向店铺的链接。** 只需把 https://gegyjiji.base.shop/ 放进 Instagram 简介，让任何喜欢某幅作品的人不必开口就能找到购买入口。这是价值最高、最省力的一步。

2. **Add one quiet line: "DM to inquire about originals."** A single calm sentence in your bio or a pinned post tells people a message is welcome. No catalog, no prices needed — just that the door is open. / **加一句安静的话："原作请私信咨询。"** 简介或置顶里一句平静的话，就告诉大家欢迎来消息。不需要目录、不需要价格——只是表明门是开着的。

3. **Let "sold" and "available" be visible.** You already mark SOLD OUT beautifully on BASE. Carrying that same small note to a caption when something sells ("this one has found a home") quietly signals that pieces are real, finite, and collectible — without any urgency. / **让"已售"和"在售"可见。** 你在 BASE 上的 SOLD OUT 标记已经很好。卖出时在配文里加同样的小注脚（"这幅已经有了归宿"），就安静地表明作品是真实、有限、可收藏的——毫无紧迫感。

4. **(Optional) A simple price list, once.** If you ever want to, a single pinned post or highlight with three calm tiers — postcards from ¥1,100, zines around ¥1,980, originals from ¥31,900 — answers the most common question before it's asked. Optional, and easy to skip. / **（可选）一份简单的价目表，做一次就好。** 如果你想，可以用一条置顶或精选，列出三个平静的档位——明信片 ¥1,100 起、画册约 ¥1,980、原作 ¥31,900 起——在被问到之前就答好了最常见的问题。可选，跳过也完全没关系。

5. **(Optional) A second storefront only if it feels easy.** Creema or BOOTH can hold your zines and postcards for buyers who never see your Instagram. Set it up only on a day it feels light — there's no rush and no penalty for never doing it. / **（可选）只有在感觉轻松时再开第二家店。** Creema 或 BOOTH 可以为没看过你 Instagram 的买家承载画册和明信片。只在感觉轻松的某天去做就好——不必着急，永远不做也没有任何损失。

## 3. Format and price guidance, grounded in your real catalog

This is descriptive, not prescriptive — it's just what your own sales already tell us.

这是描述，而非规定——只是你自己的销售记录已经告诉我们的事。

- **Your originals are valued correctly at the top end.** Three originals at ¥115,500 have **sold out.** That is real, demonstrated demand at your highest price — not a fluke, and not a number to second-guess or discount. The market has already confirmed that collectors will pay your top rate for the right piece. / **你的原作在高端定价是准确的。** 三幅 ¥115,500 的原作已经**售罄**。这是在你最高价位上真实、已被证明的需求——不是偶然，也不是需要怀疑或打折的数字。市场已经确认：遇到对的作品，收藏者愿意付你的最高价。

- **The accessible tier is doing its job.** Zines (~¥1,980) and postcard sets (¥1,100–¥1,980) are the easy entry point — the way a new follower becomes a first-time buyer, and a first-time buyer eventually becomes an original collector. Keeping this tier alive is what feeds the top tier over time. / **亲民档位正在发挥作用。** 画册（约 ¥1,980）和明信片套装（¥1,100–¥1,980）是轻松的入口——新粉丝由此成为第一次购买者，而第一次购买者最终可能成为原作收藏者。让这个档位持续存在，正是在为高端档位"养水"。

- **(Optional) The one gap is a middle tier.** Right now there is a large jump between a ¥1,980 zine and a ¥31,900 original, and you don't currently sell prints. *If you ever want one,* a small open-edition print line (giclée reproductions of favourite pieces, perhaps ¥3,000–¥8,000) would let someone who loves a sold original still own that image. This is purely optional — many artists deliberately never make prints to keep originals singular, and that is a completely valid choice. Listed only so the option is visible. / **（可选）唯一的空档是中间档位。** 目前 ¥1,980 的画册和 ¥31,900 的原作之间跨度很大，而你现在没有卖版画。*如果你想，* 一条小型开放版数的版画线（把心爱作品做成艺术微喷，约 ¥3,000–¥8,000）能让喜欢某幅已售原作的人仍然拥有那个画面。这纯属可选——许多艺术家刻意从不做版画，以保持原作的唯一性，那也是完全成立的选择。列在这里只是让这个选项可见。
