## 3. Long bio (~185 words)
GEGYjiji is a Chinese watercolor artist based between Tokyo and Beijing. Born in Changsha, in Hunan Province, she studied illustration and design at the Beijing Institute of Fashion Technology (BIFT), and her practice remains rooted in the illustration and artist-book community as much as in the gallery.

Since 2020 she has maintained a daily watercolor diary — a continuous series simply called *diary* — from which her first solo collection, *Colour Diary* (2021), grew directly. She paints the ordinary and the overlooked: interior light moving across a room, a cat settled in a warm corner, a street after rain, the particular grey-green of a Tokyo afternoon. Working between two cities, her paintings hold China and Japan at once, with watercolor's transparency letting atmosphere and memory accumulate in layers.

She has exhibited across China, Japan, and the United Kingdom since 2021, including two museum group exhibitions, her first solo show in Shanghai (*Light, Shadow, Cats*, 2025), and a solo exhibition in Tokyo (*The Eternal Yesterday*, Tsuki Gallery, 2026). Her work has been featured twice by *Bored Panda*, and she shares a daily watercolor practice with an established, growing following on Instagram.

## 4. Artist statement (~150 words)
I paint the ordinary. Since 2020 I have kept a daily watercolor diary — small studies of the places I move through, made the way you might write a single sentence at the end of a day. My subjects are quiet ones: light falling through a window, a cat asleep on a warm floor, a street corner after rain, the grey-green hour particular to Tokyo. I am Chinese, and I live between Beijing and Tokyo, so my work holds two cities at once — red walls and alleyways, pools and green ponds, rooms I half-remember. Watercolor suits this slow looking; its transparency lets atmosphere accumulate in layers, the way memory does. Like a train traveling through the north, the paintings carry all four seasons. I want the viewer to feel they are walking through somewhere familiar — to find an ordinary day turned, for a moment, into something worth keeping.

## 9. How to use this kit (English)
A press kit isn't homework — it's a folder you build once and then simply *have ready*. When someone asks for materials, you answer in one message instead of writing from scratch.

1. **Keep it ready.** Save this as a PDF (and keep the editable file). When a gallery, magazine, publisher, or shop asks "do you have a press kit?", you reply and attach it. Nothing new to write in the moment.
2. **Pick the language and the length.** Send the **Japanese** version to Japanese press and galleries, **Chinese** as your default, **English** for international. Most people only need the short bio + fact sheet + a few images — you rarely send everything.
3. **Attach 3–5 images, not the whole portfolio.** Choose the strongest works for that specific reader (a cats-and-interiors feature gets *Tokyo Still Life*; a city or architecture piece gets *Urban Atmosphere*). Use the caption format so credits travel with the files.
4. **Add one warm line of your own.** The kit is the formal part; a single personal sentence — "I loved your recent feature on ___ and thought my work might suit it" — makes it human. Then send. That's the whole job.

## 9. 使用说明（简体中文）
新闻资料包不是作业——它是你只需做一次、之后随时「备好」的文件夹。有人来要资料时，你只需回一条消息，不用从头写。

1. **随时备好。** 把它存成 PDF（并保留可编辑的源文件）。当画廊、杂志、出版方或店铺问「你有新闻资料包吗？」时，你回复并附上即可，当下无需再写。
2. **选择语言与篇幅。** 把**日文版**发给日本媒体与画廊，**中文版**作为你的默认，**英文版**用于海外。多数人只需要短简介 + 资料速览 + 几张图片——很少需要全部。
3. **附 3–5 张图，而非整个作品集。** 为对方挑选最合适的作品（讲猫与室内的报道用《东京静物》；讲城市或建筑的用《都市氛围》）。使用图注格式，让署名随文件一起传递。
4. **加一句你自己的暖心话。** 资料包是正式的部分；一句私人化的话——「我很喜欢你们最近关于 ___ 的报道，觉得我的作品也许合适」——会让它有人情味。然后发送即可。这就完成了。

## 10. How to keep it updated (English)
Short and low-pressure — a few minutes, only when something real changes.

- After each show or publication, add **one line** to Selected Exhibitions / Publications (newest first). Two minutes.
- Refresh the **artist statement** only when your work genuinely shifts — not on a schedule.
- **Swap in newer images** as you make stronger work; quietly retire older ones.
- Leave the audience line as "**established, growing**" — no need to chase a number.
- **Re-export the PDF** whenever you change something, and keep one master file so there's only ever one true version.

## 10. 如何保持更新（简体中文）
简短、无压力——只在确有变化时花几分钟。

- 每办一次展或出一次刊，就在「精选展览 / 出版物」里加**一行**（最新的放最前）。两分钟。
- **艺术家自述**只在创作真正转向时更新——不必按时间表来。
- 有更强的新作就**替换新图**，悄悄淘汰旧的。
- 受众那行保持「**稳定增长**」即可——不必追逐具体数字。
- 每次改动后**重新导出 PDF**，并只保留一份主文件，确保始终只有一个真实版本。
