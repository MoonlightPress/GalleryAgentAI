Across these inventories, **310 units receive a rewrite, removal, scope correction or hold**, with additional interface and row-level changes in documents 05–06. Retaining wording is not an endorsement of an unverified figure.

The most consequential findings are the Chinese/English calendar mismatch, the book cash-recovery formula, the scope of quoted illustration fees, unsupported claims about her sales/audience, and stale or overconfident eligibility statements. These need underlying corrections alongside the language pass.

“Hold” and “verify” drafts are not final answers to publish in place of research. They identify where a confident sentence currently exceeds its evidence. Complete the relevant research before promoting those entries as recommendations; correct English wherever the underlying claim changes.

The supporting scripts and JSON snapshots make the structured audit traceable. They operate only in this review folder.
