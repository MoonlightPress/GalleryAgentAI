## Who I Am

I am GEGYjiji — 26, painting watercolours in Tokyo, and my brain is autistic *and* ADHD at once. That pairing is not "twice as scattered." It means I go all the way down into one thing when the thing is right (I can lose a whole afternoon to a single roofline and call it the best day of the month), and I stall completely when I'm asked to choose. Task *initiation* is my wall, not effort. A list of 419 things is not abundance to me; it is a locked door with 419 keys and no label on any of them. I will read every card, feel the day drain out of me, and close the tab having done nothing — and then feel ashamed of that, which makes opening it tomorrow harder.

What creates calm for me is *predictability and quiet*: a screen that ends, one clear next move, plain affordances, no flicker, no horizontal surprise, no foreign words appearing in my Chinese. What creates flow is a room that lets me hyperfocus — that doesn't keep tugging me sideways into a second tab and a fifth sub-tab. What creates shutdown is sensory pile-up (a feed that scrolls for eight thousand pixels on my laptop, fifteen thousand on my phone), and what creates outright *refusal* is anything that reads as a quota, a "should," a denominator, or a verdict. A red word judging my month, a "2/3" with a progress bar, a count of my rejections — my nervous system reads those as a hand on my collar, and I pull away whether or not pulling away is good for my career.

So I am not the easy user. I am the one this dashboard's gold standard — *calm, optional, affirming, never rank/quota/pressure/shame* — was actually written for. For me that ethos is not a nicety; it is the difference between a tool I open and a tab I avoid. And it has to hold on both screens: the big screen where I'll meet it first, and the phone where I'll actually live with it.

## What Calms / Serves Me

**Saffron's collapse-by-default, and how well it uses the big screen.** On the laptop, 概况 / 人脉与媒体 / 收入 open with their heavy sections folded (I counted 6 and 5 collapsed toggles), and the strategy renders as three tidy columns instead of a stack. That is the single best thing here for a mind like mine: I get to choose what unfolds, and the wide screen lets me *see one section in context* without the others shouting. This is monotropism-friendly in a way the mobile stack can't quite match — and it's a real argument for Scott's "meet it on the big screen first" instinct.

**The 今日重点 pair with effort labels** (⚡快速行动 · 5分钟) is genuinely good for executive-function trouble, on both screens. Naming the *cost* of an action ("5 minutes") is exactly what lets me start one. A 5-minute door I walk through; an unlabelled door I stand in front of forever.

**The register, where it holds, lands on me bodily**: 策略's "没有截止日期，也不催你——想做的时候再做," Peppercorn's "这里没有急事，我会记住一切," the absence of any fail state. When the copy refuses to push, I stop bracing. There is a real, kind dashboard in here. It's just buried under load and undercut by a few counting surfaces.

## What Overwhelms or Wounds Me

**DESIGN problems (intent itself is wrong for my mind) — these hurt on BOTH screens:**

1. **No single surface that says "do this one thing today" — and then ends.** The worst one. 今日重点 gives me *two* items and then drops me into the wall (8,021px laptop / 15,245px phone); the calm 下一步 lives in a *different tab* (Saffron 策略). My "what do I do now" is split across two tabs, which for a monotropic brain is a context-switch tax paid in lost-place and abandoned sessions. The "rich on knowing, thin on doing" finding is the exact crack I fall through.

2. **The Discover chips are anchors, not filters, so I can never quiet the room.** I confirmed the wall stays full-height no matter which chip I tap (laptop: 8,021px unchanged; phone: 15,245px unchanged). The inability to see *one category at a time* and hide the rest is the difference between usable and aversive — on both viewports.

3. **Quota/denominator framing in the Peppercorn carousel.** Live on both screens: "来自山楂的提问 **0/8**", "职业目标 **2/3**", each with a fill bar, plus "**分析依据不完整**" and "山楂用这些来调整推荐的权重". A denominator on *optional* prompts is a "should"; deficit + "adjust the weights" is surveillance. My PDA refuses all of it.

4. **Quantified zeros and a latent verdict (Momentum, 概况).** "0 投递总数 / 0 收到回复 / 响应率" reads, literally, as counted failure; "0个活跃关系" too. And the latent red **停滞** verdict (not firing today, but it will on a quiet school month) is a one-word judgment my rejection-sensitivity can't un-see.

**CURRENT-STATE problems (intent fine; today's build hurts me):**

5. **The fully-expanded, lopsided, English-leaking Career Position CV — worst on the LAPTOP.** This is the desktop-weighted wound: 职业定位 opens expanded with 12 dense rows, the right column ~60% empty, and English type/note/date fragments threaded through my zh ("Group show ·", "Institutional (museum) group exhibition", "March–April 2021"), plus "SARAH ANDERSEN" as a title and "defined Definition 02". On the big screen this is a wide slab of dense, error-speckled text — exactly the visual load I came to the laptop to escape.

6. **Calendar horizontal side-scroll — MOBILE-specific.** 日历 is 840px wide on a 375px phone (465px of overflow); on the laptop it has zero horizontal overflow. So this one is a phone wound only — but a severe one there, because unpredictable horizontal motion dysregulates me.

7. **English leaks in my zh room, both screens.** The People buckets render "High priority / Worth reaching out to / Keep on the radar"; Career Position leaks English (above); CRM notes leak English. A foreign token unbidden in my calm Chinese breaks the predictability I rely on.

## The Five Changes I'd Beg For

1. **Build a real "今天就做这一件事" surface — ONE action, that ends — on both screens.** *Change:* promote 今日重点 to a single highest-leverage card (nearest gentle deadline *or* one overdue follow-up *or* the next 下一步) with its effort label, and a clear bottom edge before the feed. *Reasoning:* task-initiation, not information, is my wall; two items split across two tabs is still a choice, and choice is where I stall. *Evidence:* 今日重点 shows "2 件" then 8,021px (laptop)/15,245px (phone); the calm 下一步 lives in a separate tab. *Falsification:* on both viewports a first session reaches exactly one labelled next action above the fold with the feed optional below, and a usability proxy completes one action without entering the wall.

2. **Let the Discover chips actually filter (and default to a short view), at every width.** *Change:* make 公开征集 / 比赛奖项 / etc. render only that category and hide the rest. *Reasoning:* monotropism + sensory load — I need to see one thing and quiet everything else; anchors that keep all 419 cards mounted give me no off-switch on either screen. *Evidence:* tapping 公开征集 left `scrollHeight` unchanged (8,021px laptop / 15,245px phone), only scroll position moved. *Falsification:* selecting a chip drops `scrollHeight` to that category's height alone at both 1280 and 375; "show all" is an explicit opt-in.

3. **Drop the denominators, fill bars, deficit and "weights" lines from the Peppercorn carousel.** *Change:* "0/8"→ a soft count or nothing; remove the `/3` fill; "分析依据不完整"→ "多说一点，胡椒粒就更懂你"; cut "用这些来调整推荐的权重". *Reasoning:* every "X/N" with a progress bar is a quota my PDA refuses; deficit + "weights" reads as surveillance — identical on both screens. *Evidence:* live carousel renders "0/8", "2/3", "分析依据不完整", "山楂用这些来调整推荐的权重". *Falsification:* no `/N` denominator, no progress-fill, no "incomplete/weights" language remains on any carousel card at any width.

4. **Tame Career Position: collapse it by default, balance the grid, and purge its English — this is the laptop-first fix.** *Change:* fold 职业定位 behind a ▾ like its neighbours (show a 2–3 line synopsis collapsed); on desktop, span the 12 exhibitions across both columns or pair Publications+Audience so the right side isn't ~60% blank; localize the type/note/date strings to zh. *Reasoning:* the dense expanded slab with a lopsided blank column and English fragments is the single heaviest desktop surface, and the big screen is where she meets it first. *Evidence:* `laptop_careerposition.png` — 12 rows, blank right column, "Group show ·"/"Institutional (museum) group exhibition"/"March–April 2021"/"SARAH ANDERSEN"/"defined Definition 02" all in zh view. *Falsification:* on the laptop, Career Position is collapsed by default, neither column is >40% empty when expanded, and no Latin-script type/note/date string renders in zh.

5. **Fix the mobile calendar overflow, purge stray English elsewhere, and never show a verdict or bare zero-failure stat (both screens).** *Change:* single-column calendar under ~560px with `min-width:0`/`overflow-wrap`; add `people.group.*` zh keys (优先联系 / 值得联系 / 保持关注); retire red + the word 停滞 from Momentum and suppress "0 投递/0 收到回复/响应率/0个活跃关系" in favour of the warm empty-state. *Reasoning:* horizontal side-scroll (phone), foreign words, a verdict, and counted zeros each break the predictability/safety my nervous system needs. *Evidence:* 日历 = 840px vs 375px on phone (0 overflow on laptop); People buckets render "High priority" etc.; Momentum renders "0 投递/0 收到回复/响应率"; `stalling:'#b03020'` latent. *Falsification:* at 375px `body.scrollWidth === clientWidth` on 日历; no Latin UI label in zh; no red career tag and no "0 …" failure metric can render — a quiet month shows only the gentle note.

## What This Could Be For Someone Like Me

If it got this right, this would be the first career tool I didn't have to recover from. Most are spreadsheets that yell — counts, deadlines, gaps, all at once — and I bounce off every one. This one already *knows the voice*: "没有截止日期，也不催你," "这里没有急事，我会记住一切," collapse-by-default so I'm never shouted at, effort labels that tell me a thing is only five minutes. Those aren't decorations to me; they're the accommodations that let an AuDHD artist use software at all. And the laptop already proves the calm version is possible — Saffron's three-column, folded layout is what the *whole* product should feel like.

The dream version is small, not big, and it's the same on both screens. I open it — on the train on my phone, or at my desk on the laptop — and one card says: *here is the single best thing to do today, it takes five minutes, want to?* — and nothing else demands me. When I have energy, I choose to unfold a section, go deep, hyperfocus, and the room stays quiet around that focus instead of fracturing me across three tabs and five sub-tabs. Nothing counts me. Nothing judges my slow month. The quiet corner (Peppercorn) really is quiet — a place I tell things, not a place that grades whether I've told enough.

That tool wouldn't just be *usable* for a mind like mine. It would feel built by someone who understood that for me, calm isn't comfort — it's access. It would mean I keep showing up, in five-minute doors, and a career gets built out of the days I didn't close the tab.

## One-Line Verdict
On the big screen the calm version is already visible (folded Saffron, three columns) and on the phone the warmth survives — but on BOTH, until there is ONE labelled next action that ends, a feed I can actually quiet, a Career Position that doesn't dump a dense English-speckled slab on me, and no quotas/zeros/verdicts counting me, the room is still too loud to stay in, and I'll close the tab I most wanted to keep open.
